These walkthroughs show the actual Enconvo interface. Steps are paused for readability, and the accompanying guides contain the same instructions in text. Example drafts are discarded after capture.

## Draft a scheduled Agent job

Fill the name and instruction, switch to Weekly, choose weekdays, and compare Monthly. The draft is discarded; no job is created.

[Read the guide and watch the video](/workflows/scheduled-jobs#watch-the-steps).

## Browse the Avatar gallery

Switch to Downloaded, search for Tia, inspect Optional downloads, then return to Gallery. No package is downloaded and no assignment is changed.
