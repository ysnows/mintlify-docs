These walkthroughs show the actual Enconvo interface. Steps are paused for readability, and the accompanying guides contain the same instructions in text. Example drafts are discarded after capture.

## Pair your iPhone

A narrated tutorial: sign in on the iPhone app, show the pairing code on your Mac, scan it, and continue your Mac's chats from your phone.

[Read the guide and watch the video](/integrations/iphone-app).
