Every guide in these docs, grouped the way the sidebar groups them. Find what you want to do, then open its guide. For individual built-in commands, see the [Command Catalog](/extensions/built-in-extensions). For models, voices, and other services, see the [Provider Catalog](/providers/catalog).

Some features reach the Beta channel first. If a guide describes something your app doesn't have, check your [update channel](/configuration/updates).
