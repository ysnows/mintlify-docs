Use your Google Antigravity plan as an AI Model provider in Enconvo. You sign in with your Google account; no API key is involved.

<Warning>
Enconvo signs in with the Antigravity app's own Google client, which Google doesn't offer to other apps. Google may suspend an Antigravity account it sees used outside Antigravity. Use an account you can afford to lose.
</Warning>

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider">
    Open **Settings → AI Model → Antigravity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the sign-in">
    In **Authentication**, click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the warning">
    Enconvo shows **Before you sign in to Antigravity**. Read the risk, then click **Agree** to continue. **Cancel** closes it and leaves your account as it was.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Your browser opens Google's sign-in. Choose the account and allow access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the account">
    Return to Enconvo and check that the intended account is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Select a model and try a short request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Enconvo asks each time you connect or reconnect Antigravity, from the provider's page or from its credential.

## Models and options

Antigravity offers the Gemini and Claude models on your plan. Each model shows the reasoning effort levels Antigravity offers for it.

## On the iPhone

The iPhone app can sign in to Antigravity too, for Phone chats on the phone. It asks you to agree to the same risk first. See [Phone Chats](/integrations/iphone-phone-chats).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The sign-in doesn't finish | Finish Google's sign-in in the browser, then return to Enconvo; click **Connect** to try again. |
| Google says the account is restricted | The account may have been suspended for use outside Antigravity; use another account or another provider. |
| The model you want isn't listed | Your Antigravity plan doesn't include it. |
| Rate or usage limit | Your plan's limits; wait for them to reset or choose another provider. |
