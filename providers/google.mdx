---
title: "Google Gemini"
description: "Chat with Gemini models in Enconvo using a Gemini API key."
---

Use Google's Gemini models in chats, Agents, and AI Commands. Connect with a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio; Google bills the usage to your own account. It's listed as **Google Gemini AI** in Settings.

<Note>
Google Gemini connects with an API key only. Google stopped supporting the Gemini sign-in for personal Google accounts, so Enconvo removed the Google Account option in 2.5.8. To use models through a Google sign-in, see [Antigravity](/providers/antigravity).
</Note>

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an API key">
    Create a key in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Enconvo">
    Open **Settings → AI Model → Google Gemini AI**. Under **Authentication**, paste the key into **API Key**, then click **Validate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Choose a model in **Model Name** and send a short message. A reply means the connection works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To keep a second Gemini key, click **Add New Provider** and create another profile.

## Models and options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Model Name** | The model this provider uses. **Refresh** beside it reloads the list; **Manage Models** (the gear) adds or restores models. |
| **Google Search Tool** | Grounds answers in Google Search results, with sources you can check. Off by default. |
| **URL Context Tool** | Lets the model read the pages at links in your message. Off by default. |
| **Show thoughts** | Shows summaries of the model's thinking with its answer. Off by default. |

The same Google Gemini profile also serves Gemini text-to-speech, image generation, transcription, video generation, and web search, so changing its key affects those features too.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Validate** fails | Paste the whole key again and check that it is active in Google AI Studio. |
| A model is missing | Click **Refresh** beside **Model Name**. To add a model yourself, use **Manage Models**. |
| Answers about current events are out of date | Turn on **Google Search Tool**. |
| Rate or usage limit errors | Check your quota in Google AI Studio, or switch to another provider for now. |
