A provider is the service behind an Enconvo feature: the model that answers a chat, the voice that reads aloud, the engine that searches the web. You choose one for each feature, and you can mix them.

The video below walks through **Settings → AI Model** in 2 minutes: Auto Route, the Cloud plan, signing in with a ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Antigravity, or Grok plan, API keys, models on your Mac, Ollama and LM Studio, and adding a provider of your own.

## Choose your chat model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Model">
    Open **Settings → AI Model**. The middle column lists every provider: **Auto Route** and **Enconvo Cloud Plan** at the top, then the companies, local models, and every other provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a provider and connect it">
    Click a provider. Where it offers both, choose **API Key** to pay the provider per request with your own key, or the subscription option (for example **ChatGPT Subscription**) to sign in and use what your plan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the model">
    Pick a model under **Model Name**, and a **Reasoning Effort** where the provider has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it the default">
    Click **Set as Default**. Chats and features that don't name a model of their own now use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add a service that isn't listed, click **Add New Provider** at the bottom of the list and choose **OpenAI Compatible** or **Anthropic Compatible**.

You can switch providers at any time; nothing ties your chats to one company.

## Set a provider for each feature

Each feature has its own Settings page: **AI Model**, **Text-to-Speech**, **Web Search**, **Image Generation**, **Video Generation**, **Web Fetch**, and **OCR**. Speech recognition has separate pages for **Dictation Models**, **Recording Models**, **Transcription Models**, and **Captioning Model**. Embeddings and reranking are set in the Knowledge Base.

Choosing a chat model doesn't change your speech, image, or search provider. Set each one where you want it.

## Profiles

A provider profile holds one key or sign-in. Click **Add New Provider** on a provider's page to keep a second one, for example a work account. Some profiles serve several features: one OpenAI profile covers chat, embeddings, text-to-speech, images, and transcription, so changing its key changes all of them. See [Credentials and profiles](/configuration/credentials).

Where your default model is set, and how each chat keeps its own, is in [Choose a model](/ai/model-selection-guide).
