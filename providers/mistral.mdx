---
title: "Mistral AI"
description: "Chat with Mistral models in Enconvo using a Mistral API key or your Mistral account."
---

Use Mistral's models in chats, Agents, and AI Commands. Paste a Mistral API key, or sign in with your Mistral account and let Enconvo create the key for you. Mistral bills the usage to your account either way.

## Connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sign in with Mistral">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the provider">
        Open **Settings → AI Model → Mistral AI**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Under **Authentication**, choose the Mistral account sign-in and click **Connect**. Sign in with your Mistral account and choose a workspace. The sign-in works the way Mistral Vibe signs in: it creates an API key for that workspace, so there's nothing to copy.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick a model">
        Choose a model in **Model Name** and send a short message.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Use an API key">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Get an API key">
        Create a key in the [Mistral console](https://console.mistral.ai/api-keys/).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add it to Enconvo">
        Open **Settings → AI Model → Mistral AI**. Under **Authentication**, paste the key into **API Key**, then click **Validate**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick a model">
        Choose a model in **Model Name** and send a short message.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once you're signed in, the account card shows your plan, the key, and what's left of your per-minute limits.

## Models and options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Model Name** | The model this provider uses. **Refresh** beside it reloads the list; **Manage Models** (the gear) adds or restores models. |
| **Reasoning Effort** (in the chat) | For Mistral Large 4, Medium 3.5, and Small 4: **None** answers without thinking, **High** thinks first. Large 4 thinks by default. |

Voxtral transcription and Mistral OCR can use the same sign-in or key.

## On the iPhone

The iPhone app can use your Le Chat plan in Phone chats too. On the iPhone, open **Settings → AI Model → Mistral**, choose the Le Chat card under **Authentication**, and tap **Sign in with Mistral**. See [Phone Chats](/integrations/iphone-phone-chats).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Validate** fails | Paste the whole key again and check that it is active in the Mistral console. |
| The sign-in stops working | Open the account card's menu and click **Reconnect**. |
| A model is missing | Click **Refresh** beside **Model Name**. To add a model yourself, use **Manage Models**. |
| Rate limit errors | Your workspace's per-minute limit is used up. Wait a minute, or check limits in the Mistral console. |
