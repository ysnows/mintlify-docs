---
title: "Claude Subscription"
description: "Sign in with your Claude plan to use Claude models in Enconvo without an API key."
---

If you pay for Claude, sign in with that plan instead of creating an API key. Requests use your plan's allowance, and Enconvo shows how much is left.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the subscription">
    Open **Settings → AI Model → Anthropic**. Under **Authentication**, choose **Claude Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to your Claude account in the browser, and allow access. Come back to Enconvo when the browser says you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Check that the account card shows the right account, choose a model in **Model Name**, and send a short message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check your usage

The account card shows how much of the five-hour and weekly allowances is left (**% left**), per model when Anthropic reports it, and when each resets. Open the card's menu for:

- **Refresh usage**: fetch the latest numbers.
- **Reconnect**: sign in again, for example to switch accounts.

When Anthropic doesn't report usage, the card says **Usage unavailable** with the reason. That doesn't mean the plan has no limit.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in fails | Read the error Anthropic gave, then click **Connect** again. Make sure you sign in to the account that has the plan. |
| A chat says a usage limit was reached | The message names the limit and when it resets. Wait, or switch **Authentication** to **API Key** for now. |
| Enconvo asks you to try again in a moment | Anthropic was busy renewing the sign-in. Wait a moment and send again; you don't need to reconnect. |
| A model is missing | Your plan decides the models. Click **Refresh** beside **Model Name**. |
