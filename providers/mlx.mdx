---
title: "Local LLM (MLX)"
description: "Run language models on your Mac with Enconvo's built-in MLX engine."
---

Local LLM runs language models on your Mac's Apple Silicon, with no account or API key. After the model downloads, chats with it stay on your Mac. It needs macOS 15 or later.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider">
    Open **Settings → AI Model → Local LLM (MLX)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Open the **Model** list. Enconvo suggests a model that suits your Mac, and you can search Hugging Face for others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Start the download and wait for it to finish. Enconvo checks free disk space first, and a download resumes after a restart or a network drop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Send a short message. The first reply takes longer while the model loads into memory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Models and options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Model** | The local model this provider runs. |
| **Thinking** | For models with a thinking mode. **Off** (the default) answers right away; **On** reasons first. **Low**, **Medium**, and **High** set how long gpt-oss models reason; other models treat them as **On**. |

To see how much memory and disk your local models use, or to delete one, open **Settings → Local Models**. Models load again when a feature needs them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The download doesn't start | Free up disk space; Enconvo checks it before downloading. |
| The model fails to load or the Mac slows down | Pick a smaller model, or close other heavy apps. **Settings → Local Models** shows memory use. |
| Replies are slow | Pick a smaller model, or turn **Thinking** off. |
| Tools or images fail | Pick a model that supports tool calls or image input. |
