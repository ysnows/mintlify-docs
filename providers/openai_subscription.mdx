---
title: "ChatGPT Subscription"
description: "Sign in with your ChatGPT plan to use OpenAI models in Enconvo without an API key."
---

If you pay for ChatGPT, sign in with that plan instead of creating an API key. Requests use your plan's allowance, and Enconvo shows how much is left.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the subscription">
    Open **Settings → AI Model → OpenAI**. Under **Authentication**, choose **ChatGPT Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to your ChatGPT account in the browser, and allow access. Come back to Enconvo when the browser says you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Check that the account card shows the right account, choose a model in **Model Name**, and send a short message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Enconvo renews the sign-in in the background, so you don't need to sign in again every few days.

## Check your usage

The account card shows how much of each usage window is left (**% left**) and when it resets, plus when the plan renews or ends. Open the card's menu for:

- **Refresh usage**: fetch the latest numbers.
- **Reconnect**: sign in again, for example to switch accounts.

When OpenAI doesn't report usage, the card says **Usage unavailable** with the reason. That doesn't mean the plan has no limit.

## What the plan covers

The ChatGPT sign-in also works for OpenAI image generation and transcription; choose **ChatGPT Subscription** under **Authentication** on those pages. OpenAI text-to-speech and embeddings need an API key, and Live Talk voices are set up separately.

## On the iPhone

The iPhone app has its own ChatGPT sign-in for Phone chats. On the iPhone, open **Settings → AI Model → OpenAI**, choose the ChatGPT card under **Authentication**, and tap **Sign in with ChatGPT**. The page then shows your plan, how much of its 5-hour and weekly limits is left, and when each resets. That sign-in stays on the iPhone; the Mac keeps its own. See [Phone Chats](/integrations/iphone-phone-chats).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in fails | Read the error OpenAI gave, then click **Connect** again. Make sure you sign in to the account that has the plan. |
| A model is missing | Your plan decides the models. Click **Refresh** beside **Model Name**. |
| A chat says a usage limit was reached | Wait for the reset time on the account card, or switch **Authentication** to **API Key** for now. |
| Chats stop working after a while | Open the account card's menu and click **Reconnect**. |
