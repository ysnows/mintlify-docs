title: "Email"
description: "Read, sort, and answer your own mailboxes in the Chat Window, with Today, Ask Mavis, drafted replies, and alerts on your phone."
---

Email is a plugin that comes with Enconvo. Add the mailboxes you own, and read, search, and answer them in the **Chat Window**, with a summary and suggested replies on each conversation. Agents can search and draft in your mailboxes too, and always ask before they send or delete anything.

Email updates from the Plugin Store like other plugins. Uninstalling it removes your mailbox passwords and saved mail from this Mac.

## Add a mailbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email">
    In the **Chat Window** sidebar, select **Email**, then **Add Mailbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where your mail lives">
    Choose your mail provider. For any other mail server, select **Enter Server Settings** and fill in the **Incoming** and **Outgoing** server, **Port**, and **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter your address and password, or an **App password** if your provider asks for one. Some providers sign you in in your browser instead. The password is kept in this Mac's keychain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Accounts you've connected in the [Gmail](/integrations/gmail) plugin and in Apple Mail show up on the left by themselves.

On a mailbox's **Accounts** page you can also:

- Add **Also sends from** addresses, so you can send from your aliases. A reply goes out from the address its mail came to.
- Turn **Let agents read this mailbox** off to keep agents out of it. It's on as soon as a mailbox is connected.
- Set the mailbox's automations: **Read new mail and notify my phone**, and **Include in the morning report**.
- Select **Remove Mailbox**.

## Today

Email opens on **Today**: an overview of how much needs you today, with the most urgent senders and each mailbox's count. Below it, mail is sorted into tiers:

| Tier | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Urgent · must reply** | Mail you need to answer now |
| **Due today** | Mail with a deadline today, including one picked up from its text |
| **Waiting on others** | Replies you're waiting for. A mail turns amber after 3 days. |
| **Worth a look** | Mail worth reading but not urgent |
| **Later** | Newsletters and notifications, folded into one row you can archive all at once, and undo |

Each mail says why it's there and offers one next step, such as **Draft Reply**, **Review Draft**, **Open**, or **Nudge**, plus **Snooze** and **Archive**.

Open a mail to go through its tier one by one with **Previous** and **Next** and a count of how many are done. After you send, archive, or snooze, the next mail opens by itself.

Rules sort what they can right away, such as a question from outside that's been waiting a day, replies you're waiting on, and notifications and promotions. The model reads each new mail only once.

## Read a conversation

A conversation shows its subject at the top, which moves into the top bar as you scroll, and a one-line **Summary**. Each mail has a full header: the sender's name and address, the full date and time, and whom it went to and was copied to. Long recipient lists fold behind **+N more**.

The toolbar has **Ask Mavis**, **Archive**, and a **More** menu with **Flag**, **Snooze**, **Mark as Unread**, **Reply All**, **Forward**, **Move**, **Junk**, and **Delete**. Archive and Junk can be undone, and an archived conversation has **Move to Inbox**.

### Ask Mavis

Select **Ask Mavis**, or press **⌘J**, to open a panel beside the mail. Ask about the mail and follow up. Answers name the mails and attachments they come from, are kept with the conversation for next time, and follow you to the next mail you open.

- Attach files and images by picking, dragging, or pasting them, or add a Screen Doodle, and Mavis answers from them along with the mail.
- **Use as Draft** writes the answer into the reply field, marked **Draft from Mavis · not sent**, for you to check and send yourself.
- **Continue in Chat** carries the questions and answers into the chat beside Email.

## Write and reply

Type in the reply field (**Reply to …**) and select **Send**. Or let the AI help:

- Select a suggested reply, or **Draft a Reply**, to draft an answer in one press.
- **Polish** turns rough notes into a finished mail, the way a native speaker would write it.
- **Describe a change** makes any other edit you ask for. **Undo** brings your own words back.
- A reply that still has a blank to fill is stopped before it goes.

When you write, you can add **Cc** and **Bcc**, forward, pick addresses from suggestions, and drop or paste files. A mail can also go out with **Send Later**, be taken back with **Undo Send**, or come back to you if nobody replies. Use **Templates** for mail you write often.

If a mail can't reach its server, Email keeps trying for about a day and says why it's waiting, with **Try Now** and **Edit**. A mail that won't go is marked **Not sent**, and you're alerted on the Dynamic Island and your phone.

## Keep the inbox tidy

- **Snooze** a conversation until later. Snoozed mail waits under **Snoozed** until its time.
- **Sorting** rules file mail for you.
- **Subscriptions** lists your mailing lists, so you can unsubscribe.

With the sidebar folded, the bar at the top of each page holds a menu to switch folders or show the sidebar again.

## Alerts and the morning report

Two [Automations](/workflows/scheduled-jobs) come with Email:

- **New mail alerts** read new mail and tell your phone what needs you.
- The **Morning report** sums up every mailbox once a day.

Both stay quiet on a day with nothing new. New mail in Email's mailboxes can also start an automation of your own: pick new email as what starts it.

## Let agents use your mail

In a chat, type **@** and pick **Email** to work with your mail. An agent can search and draft in the mailboxes that let agents read them.

When you ask an agent to write or reply to an email, a mail card shows in the chat with its recipients, mailbox, subject, message, and files. Change anything on it; nothing goes until you select **Send**.

<Note>
Agents always ask before sending or deleting mail, unless the chat runs without asking.
</Note>
