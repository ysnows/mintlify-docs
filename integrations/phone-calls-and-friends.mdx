The Enconvo iPhone and Android apps carry your Friends, Friend Chat and Video Conference with you. Message a friend, send a voice note or a file, call them, or join a conference from a link, and keep talking while you use other apps.

## Before you start

Sign the app in to your Enconvo account. Video Conference needs a paid plan. See [Video Conference](/features/video-conference).

## Chat with friends

Open **Friends** to see the people you chat and call with. Friend Chat works as on the Mac (see [Friends and Friend Chat](/features/friends)), with files up to 1 GB, calls from the chat, **Send to**, **Report** and profile photos. A notification for a new message opens the chat.

### Groups, replies and reactions

- **Start a group**: tap **+** in the Friends list. In a group, type `@` to mention someone, and see who has seen your message. Open the group's details to rename it, change its picture, set its notifications, add or remove people, hand it on, or leave it.
- **React or reply**: hold a message to react with a quick emoji or any emoji, or to reply with a quote. Tap a reaction to add or remove yours; on the iPhone, hold it to see who reacted. Tap a quote to jump to the original message.
- **Forward or copy**: select several messages to forward or copy them together.

### Files and pictures

Videos, PDFs and text files open right in the chat; save them on the iPhone, or save and share them on Android. A tap on a picture shows it full screen inside Enconvo, where you can pinch or double-tap to zoom and swipe down to close.

### Voice messages

1. Hold **Hold to Talk** and speak. A message can be up to a minute long.
2. Let go to send it.
3. Slide to **Cancel** to throw it away, or slide to **Text** to turn it into text you can edit before sending.

## Join or start a call

- **Join from a link**: open the conference link on the phone, or tap it in a chat. It opens in the app, a **Ready** page comes up first, and you join from there.
- **Start a call**: open the **Video Conference** page, or call a friend or teammate from their chat.
- **Answer a call**: the phone rings when a friend calls you, also with the app in the background. A call you miss leaves a **Missed call** notification. On Android, answering an agent's call from the ring notification or the lock screen connects it in the background, like a phone call, without unlocking or opening the app; the ongoing call notification ends the call or opens its chat.

On the iPhone, calls run as phone calls, so music or a video in another app doesn't cut off your microphone. They start on the earpiece unless your camera is on.

## In a call

The call controls are one row of five buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Mute** | Turns your microphone off and on. |
| **Camera** | Turns your camera off and on. Its sheet flips, mirrors or hides your picture. |
| **Speaker** | Opens the **Audio** sheet, where you pick the phone, the speaker or a headset. |
| **More** | Captions, notes, sharing and the call's other options. |
| **Leave** | Leaves the call. |

A friend call goes full screen with a slim row of buttons, sideways too. **Message** opens your chat with them below their picture.

### Captions

Tap **Captions** to turn them on, and hold it to pick a language. The translation shows large with the original under it.

### Keep the call while you use other apps

Leave the app and the call keeps going in a small floating window, showing whoever was big when you left. **Switch** follows the speaker, a camera, or you. Tuck the window to the side of the screen to get it out of the way.

### Share your iPhone's screen

On the iPhone, tap **More → Share screen**. Anyone in the call can draw on it to point at what to tap, and the strokes show in a small window over whatever app you're in.

When someone else shares a screen, drag the speaker's small picture to any corner.

## Conferences on the iPhone

The iPhone app has the full conference toolset: private messages, files, organizer controls, a waiting room, Spotlight, backgrounds and masks, and **Stop incoming video**.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule, join, add to your calendar or call off a conference | Open **Coming up** in the app's Settings. |
| Get notes from a call | In **More**, turn on **Record and take notes**. Notes record on your Mac. **Open notes** shows them, and **Ask AI** summarizes the call with the iPhone's own model. |
| Hold calls while you're busy | Turn on **Do Not Disturb** in the app's **Settings → Conference**. |

Friends' messages show at the top of the call screen while you're in a call.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Calls don't show full screen on Android 14 or later | Android may have turned off full-screen notifications. Turn on **Full-screen notifications** in the app's Settings. |
