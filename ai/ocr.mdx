title: "OCR"
description: "Copy the text from any part of your screen or from an image, read on your Mac or by an online service."
---

OCR reads the text in a screenshot or an image, so you can copy it, paste it, translate it, or ask about it. Reach for it when you can't select the text, such as in an image, a video frame, or a scanned page. You choose whether the text is read on your Mac or by an online service.

## Before you start

Enconvo needs the Screen Recording permission to capture your screen. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions).

## Copy text from the screen

1. Run **Screenshot OCR** from SmartBar.
2. Drag over the area with the text.
3. The text opens in the Dynamic Island. Click **Copy**, or **Paste** (**⌘↩**) to put it in the app you're using.

From the same result you can also click **Translate** or **Ask AI**. The **⋯** menu has **Keep Original Line Breaks**, **Copy Image**, **Save Image…**, **Select Area Again**, **Add to Knowledge Base**, and **OCR Settings…**.

To read the area again with another provider, click the provider's name above the text and pick one. When the provider knows where each line is, switch to the image view to select lines on the screenshot, then click **Copy Selection**.

## Other OCR commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Screenshot OCR** | Reads a screen area and opens the text in the Dynamic Island |
| **Silent Screenshot OCR** | Reads a screen area and copies the text to the clipboard |
| **OCR** | Reads the text in an image you give it |
| **Screenshot Translate** | Translates a screen area. See [Translation](/ai/translate) |
| **Screenshot Explain** | Explains a screen area. See [Writing Toolkit](/features/writing-tools) |

## Choose a provider

Open **Settings → OCR**, pick a provider, and click **Set as Default**.

| Provider | Where the text is read |
| --- | --- |
| **Apple Native OCR** | On your Mac, with Apple's own text recognition |
| **PP-OCRv6 (MLX)** | On your Mac, with PaddleOCR detection and recognition models on Apple silicon. Nothing leaves your Mac |
| **AI Model OCR** | By the AI model you choose, following its **Instruction** |
| **Mistral OCR** | By Mistral's online service, with your Mistral key or Mistral sign-in |
| **Mistral OCR (Enconvo Cloud Plan)** | By Mistral's online service, through your Enconvo Cloud plan |

Each OCR command can also use a provider of its own: set **OCR Provider** in its settings under **Settings → Plugins → OCR**. See [Provider Catalog](/providers/catalog#ocr) for each provider's fields.

## Get better results

Capture a tight, readable area at a size where the text is easy to read. Check columns, tables, punctuation, code indentation, and characters that look alike by hand.

A scanned document can need OCR even when it's a PDF file.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The capture is blank or doesn't start | Check the Screen Recording permission in [Permissions](/configuration/permissions) |
| **No Text Found** | Click **Select Area Again** and choose a tighter area. Handwriting or stylized type may read better with **Try AI Model OCR** |
| **Couldn't Read** | Click **Retry**, try a simple image, and read the provider's error. **Settings** opens the OCR settings |
| Lines run together or break in the wrong places | Turn **Keep Original Line Breaks** on or off in the **⋯** menu, or read the area again with another provider |
| You want to ask about something on screen, not copy its text | Use [Appshots](/features/appshots) or [Screen Doodle](/features/screen-doodle) |
