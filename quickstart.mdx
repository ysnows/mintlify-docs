---

This page takes you from download to your first answer. You'll install the app, choose how Enconvo reaches AI, and ask a question from SmartBar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and open Enconvo">
    Download Enconvo from [enconvo.com](https://www.enconvo.com), open the DMG, and drag **Enconvo** into **Applications**. Open it from there. Enconvo needs macOS 14 or later. See [Installation](/installation) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how Enconvo reaches AI">
    The simplest option is to sign in to your Enconvo account. [Auto Route](/providers/auto-route), the default model, then answers on [Enconvo Cloud](/providers/enconvo). You can switch any time:

    | Option | How |
    | --- | --- |
    | Enconvo Cloud | Sign in to your Enconvo account. |
    | A plan you already pay for | Sign in with [ChatGPT](/providers/openai_subscription), [Claude](/providers/anthropic_subscription), [Grok](/providers/xai), [GitHub Copilot](/providers/github-copilot), and others. |
    | Your own API key | Open **Settings → AI Model**, pick a provider, and paste the key. |
    | A model on your Mac | Set up a [local model](/ai/local-llm). |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Press **⇧⌘D** to open SmartBar, type a question, such as asking for a three-item checklist for a productive morning, and press Return to see the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it on your own text">
    Select a paragraph in any app. When [PopBar](/features/popbar) appears next to it, click **Explain** or **Translate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
For a guided walk through SmartBar, Dictation, Voice Command, PopBar, and App Sidebar, open **Settings → General** and click **Start Tour** next to **Guide Tour**. It takes about three minutes.
</Tip>

macOS asks for permissions such as Microphone or Screen Recording the first time a feature needs them. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions) for what each one enables.

## If nothing happens

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **⇧⌘D** doesn't open SmartBar | Check the hotkey in **Settings → SmartBar**, or use the Enconvo menu bar icon. |
| SmartBar opens but there's no answer | Sign in to your Enconvo account, or check the provider in **Settings → AI Model**. |
| PopBar doesn't appear | Grant Accessibility permission. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions). |
