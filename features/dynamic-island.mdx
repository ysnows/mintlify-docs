The Dynamic Island sits at the top center of your screen. Point at it to open Home, where you can type or speak a request, attach context, run a shortcut, or go back to a task in progress. Use it for quick requests and to keep an eye on what your Agents are doing without opening a window.

For other jobs, use [SmartBar](/features/smartbar) for a quick question or command from a shortcut, the [Chat Window](/ai/chat) for long conversations and files, the [App Sidebar](/features/app-sidebar) to work beside one app, [PopBar](/features/popbar) to act on selected text, or the [Desktop Pet](/features/pet) for a character on your desktop. [Window Management](/features/window-management) compares them all.

## Start a request

1. Open **Settings → Dynamic Island** and turn on **Enable Dynamic Island**.
2. Point at the top center of the screen to open Home. To open it from the keyboard, set a **Shortcut** on the **Opening & Display** tab first.
3. Type a request, or click one of the **Home shortcuts**.
4. Check any attached context before you send. [Appshots](/features/appshots) and [Screen Doodle](/features/screen-doodle) help you ask about other apps.
5. Follow the progress, and open the result when the task finishes.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Dynamic Island**. Its settings are on three tabs.

### Home

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Home shortcuts** | The grid of shortcuts beside the input on Home. Drag a shortcut to reorder it or move it to another page, point to it and click **×** to take it off, or click **Add Shortcuts** to add more. |
| Weather and widget pages | **Add the weather page** or **Add a widget page** after your shortcuts. |
| **Default Agent** | The Agent that answers what you type on Home. You can switch Agents in the input at any time. |

**Reset Home to Default** brings back Home's default layout. Each tab has its own reset button.

### Opening & Display

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Open when you point at it** | How long you point before it opens: **Right away**, **Short pause**, or **Long pause**. |
| **Fold when you move away** | How long it stays open after the pointer leaves: **Quickly**, **Normal**, or **Slowly**. |
| **Shortcut** | Shows, opens, or folds the island from any app. |
| **Hide until you point at it** | Keeps the folded island out of sight until you point at the top center of the screen or press the shortcut. |
| **Leave the notch to other apps** | Pointing at the notch itself doesn't open the island, so other notch apps can use it. |
| **Bounce** | How much the island springs as it opens and folds. |
| **Show on** | **All Screens**, **Follow Mouse**, or one display you pick. |
| **Hide in full screen** | Hides the island on a display while an app there is full screen. |
| **Show in screenshots and recordings** | Turn it off to keep the island out of what you capture or share. |

### Character

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Show a character on the island** | Your Desktop Pet or the Default Agent's Avatar appears in the folded island and acts out what your Agents are doing. |
| **Character** | **Avatar** or **Pet**. |
| **Motion** | **Always**, **While working**, or **Never**. |
| **Use it as the agent's icon** | Shows the character as the Default Agent's icon in the Chat Window. |

## Voice and finished tasks

Voice input turns what you say into a prompt. For a spoken conversation, use [Live Talk](/features/live-voice); a Live Talk call you start from SmartBar moves to the Dynamic Island.

To have the island open by itself when a task you started there finishes, turn on **Open the Dynamic Island** in [Notifications](/configuration/notifications). That setting is separate from macOS notifications.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| It appears on another display | Check **Show on**. |
| It disappears when an app is full screen | Turn off **Hide in full screen**. |
| It's missing from a screenshot or recording | Turn on **Show in screenshots and recordings** before you change any permissions. |
| It doesn't open when you point at it | Press its shortcut. If the shortcut opens it, check **Open when you point at it** and **Leave the notch to other apps**. If nothing opens it, check **Enable Dynamic Island**. |
