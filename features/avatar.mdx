## Set up a character

1. Open **Settings → Avatar**.
2. Find a character in **Gallery** and click **Download** to get its base package.
3. When the download finishes, choose the Agent it stands for and click **Bind Avatar**. Each Agent has one Avatar, and each Avatar belongs to one Agent.
4. Choose the character's **Quality**.
5. Click **Open** to show the Agent's Avatar on your desktop.

An Avatar's calls speak with the voice set in **Settings → Live Talk → Speak**, and a voice change applies to the next call.

**Optional downloads** lists additional packages for that character, such as higher-resolution textures or motions when available. Start with the base package and choose a quality that suits your Mac.

To make your own character, click **Create a character** and pick its face, fur, and name, or click **Import file…** to add an OpenAvatar character file. To make one from a photo, see [Create a Live Photo character](#create-a-live-photo-character).

## Create a Live Photo character

A Live Photo character turns a portrait into an Avatar that stands and breathes on your desktop and appears as live video in calls.

1. Open **Settings → Avatar** and open the **Live Photo** tab.
2. Click **Create Live Photo**.
3. Start with a photo: click **Choose photo…** and pick one JPG, PNG, or WebP photo of the face you want to see in calls, up to 10 MB. Or choose **Describe it** and type a description of an invented character; Enconvo makes portraits for you to pick from.
4. Use **Frame the portrait** to place the face. Under **Backdrop**, keep the photo's own background in a rounded frame, or choose **Remove** to cut the character out.
5. Confirm that the photo is you, or that you have the person's permission to use it.
6. In the **Voice** step, choose how she sounds (see below), then create the character. Building takes a few minutes; you can close the window meanwhile.
7. Bind the character to an Agent and click **Open**, as for any Avatar.

The photo and call audio go to Atmee, an outside service in the US, which draws the video. You can have three photo characters; delete one to make another.

### Choose her voice

The **Voice** step offers:

- **Clone a voice**: record the person in place, or click **Upload instead** to use their audio or video. **Clone with** ElevenLabs on Enconvo Cloud, which has a monthly number of clones, or your own ElevenLabs API key.
- **Pick a voice**: choose an ElevenLabs voice, or one you cloned.
- **Follow the call voice**: use the voice set in **Settings → Live Talk → Speak**.

To change it later, click **Change voice** on her card.

### Use your own Atmee account

By default, Live Photo characters run on Enconvo's Atmee account, with daily minutes and the three-character limit. Choose **Use your own Atmee account** and paste an API key from Atmee Studio (**API keys**). New characters are then made in your account without those limits, and you can take in the avatars already in your Atmee account. The key is kept in [Credential Management](/configuration/credentials).

### Calls with a Live Photo character

In a call she appears as live video, with her lips following her voice. When nobody speaks for a minute, her live picture rests until you talk again. After a minute and a half she asks “Still there?”, and a minute later the call hangs up with a **Continue** button to pick it up again.

To put her on a live stream, see [Stream a Live Photo Character](/features/avatar-live-stream).

## Talk and interact

Set up [Live Talk](/features/live-voice), then use the Avatar's conversation controls to start talking. Right-click the character for voice, history, settings, and character controls. The chat bubble shows conversation text and voice activity.

You can move the character, scroll to rotate it, and Control-scroll or pinch to zoom. Clicks on the transparent margins pass through to the desktop.

## Switch characters

The desktop **Character** menu does not reassign Agents. Selecting a character assigned to another Agent opens that Agent's Avatar. An unassigned character is shown through the main Agent. To change which Agent a character belongs to, use **Settings → Avatar**.

The Avatar switch in the menu bar or the Dynamic Island opens the main Agent with its assigned character.

## Settings

Each downloaded character's card in **Settings → Avatar** has these controls:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | The Agent the character stands for. Assigning a new pair replaces their previous bindings; voice, appearance, and chat history are kept. |
| **Quality** | The rendering quality. **Minimal**, the default, uses the least GPU, CPU, and battery. **Balanced** and **Best quality** add lighting, shading, and larger textures, and use more GPU. |
| **Optional downloads** | Extra packages for the character, and package updates. |

**Natural lip sync**, above the gallery, downloads an on-device model that shapes every Avatar's lips from the voice it speaks with. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Live Desktop**, below it, puts a board behind your windows that shows what your Agents, coding sessions, and automations are doing, with a character that reacts when something needs you or goes wrong.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quality** can't be changed | Download the character and assign an Agent first. |
| **Create Live Photo** is unavailable | You have three photo characters. Delete one, or use your own Atmee account. |
| **Describe it** doesn't work | It needs an AI model. Choose one in **Settings → AI Model**, then try again. |
| A download fails | Read the error on the card, check your connection and free disk space, and click **Retry**. |
| The character is blurry when enlarged | Choose a higher **Quality**, and check **Optional downloads** for textures. |
| A call won't start | Check your [Live Talk](/features/live-voice) settings and macOS Microphone access. |
| The wrong Agent answers | Check the character's **Agent** in **Settings → Avatar**. |
