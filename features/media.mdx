title: "Media"
description: "Make, find, and refine every image, video, and audio clip in one place, with saved subjects and styles, folders, favorites, and one-click fixes."
---

**Media** is the one place for every image, video, and audio clip you make in Enconvo, whether you made it here or an Agent made it in a chat. Use it to generate pictures and clips with full control over the settings, keep a person or a product looking the same across them, and fix or reuse what you made.

It uses the providers you set in **Settings → Image Generation** and **Settings → Video Generation**. See [Image Generation](/ai/image-generation) and [Video Generation](/ai/video-generation) to set them up.

## Open Media

Click **Media** in the Chat Window sidebar. Pick **Images**, **Videos**, or **Audio** at the top.

- **All**, **Favorites**, and your folders sort what you made. Click **New folder** to add one, and use **Move to folder** on an item to file it.
- Type in **Search your media** to find an item by words from its prompt.
- Click the star to add an item to **Favorites**.

The files are also on your Mac, in `~/Enconvo/Media`.

You can also pick **Image** or **Video** under a new chat's message box. The model, settings, and what to avoid show as pills under the message box, with your recent images or videos and an **Open Media** link below them.

## Make an image or a video

1. On **Images** or **Videos**, describe what you want in the composer at the bottom.
2. Check the pills under it: the model, **Aspect ratio**, **Quality**, the number of images, and **Avoid** (what to leave out). Click a pill to change it, or **More settings** for the rest. Only the settings the chosen model takes appear.
3. Click **Generate**. The new items appear at the top as they finish.

When you ask for several takes, pick the ones to keep.

### More control for videos

- **Start frame** and **End frame**: add a picture the clip starts or ends on.
- **Add a shot**: split the video into shots, each with its own prompt and length. Enconvo joins them into one video.
- **Add an audio file**: use your own audio, or a clip from **Audio**.

## Keep a subject or style the same

Save a few photos of a person, a pet, a product, or a character as a subject, or a few pictures in the look you want as a style. Pick it when you generate, and it looks the same in every picture and video.

1. In the composer, click **Add a subject, style or reference**.
2. Choose **New from photos** under **Subject**, or **New style** under **Style**.
3. Give it a **Name**, add its **Photos**, and optionally **Describe it**.
4. Click **Save**. Pick it from the same menu the next time you generate.

## Refine what you made

Click an item to open its details: the prompt and every setting it used. From there:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reuse settings** | Fills the composer with the same prompt and settings |
| **Turn into a video** | Starts a video from this picture |
| **Upscale** | Makes a larger, sharper copy |
| **Remove background** | Cuts the subject out |
| **Relight** | Changes the light and mood |
| **Change a part** | Opens the editor to paint the area to change |
| **More fixes** | **Restore old photo**, **Change angle**, **Expand canvas**, and **Variations** |
| **Make more like this** | Makes new takes from the same setup |

Each fix makes a new version; the original stays under **Versions**. Click **Open in editor** to keep editing in the image or video editor.

## Make audio

1. Open **Audio** and pick **Music**, **Sound effect**, or **Voice**.
2. Describe the style or the sound, or type the words for a voice-over. Music can have lyrics or be **Instrumental**.
3. Click **Generate**. To use a file you have, click **Import audio file**.

To put audio on a video, open the video and click **Add audio**. Pick a clip from **Your audio** or **Import a file**, choose **Mix with its sound** or **Replace its sound**, and click **Add to video**.

<Warning>
Every image, video, and audio clip uses the provider's credits or your Enconvo Cloud points. Videos cost the most, so try a short one first.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A picture is refused as a subject or frame | Use a PNG, JPEG, or WebP picture |
| A setting you want isn't shown | The chosen model doesn't take it; pick another model |
| An item shows **Try again** | It failed; check the provider's access, then try again |
| No voices or models in **Audio** | Set up a provider that makes that kind of audio; Enconvo Cloud makes speech only, and music and sound effects need your own ElevenLabs key |
