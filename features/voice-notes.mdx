Voice Note records a spoken idea into a knowledge base so you can find it later. Use [Dictation](/features/dictation) when you want text typed into another app instead.

  <img src="/images/guides/voice-note.jpg" alt="Voice Note hotkey and destination knowledge base" />
</Frame>

## Before you start

Create or choose a [Knowledge Base](/ai/knowledge-base) for your notes. Voice Note has no hotkey until you set one.

## Set up the hotkey

1. Open **Settings → Voice Note**.
2. Click **Voice Note Hotkey** and press the shortcut you want. Choose whether you hold it or press it.
3. Set **Save to** to the knowledge base your notes go into. It starts as **Default Knowledge Base**.

## Record a note

1. Press your **Voice Note Hotkey** and say a short note. In hold mode, hold the shortcut while you speak.
2. Release the shortcut, or use the recording controls, to end the recording. The note is saved.
3. Open the destination knowledge base and review the saved item and its transcription.

## Make notes easier to find

Say the subject and useful names in the recording, for example: “Project Atlas: ask the designer for the updated onboarding icons.” Check important names, numbers, and dates in the transcript before you rely on them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The hotkey does nothing | Set a **Voice Note Hotkey**, and make sure **Enable Quick Capture** is on in **Settings → Quick Capture**. That switch turns on both the Quick Capture and Voice Note hotkeys. |
| No audio | Check Microphone permission and the selected input device. |
| The note isn't where you expected | Check **Save to** in **Settings → Voice Note**. |
| Transcription fails | Read the error and check the configured speech provider before you record the same note again. |
