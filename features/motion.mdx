A motion is a short animated video an Agent builds from scenes, text, and pictures: a product teaser, an explainer, a walkthrough, an animated chart, a logo sting, or a social clip. It plays beside the chat and in the **Artifacts** library, and exports as an MP4 with sound or as a GIF. A motion is animated with code, so words, prices, and interface details come out exactly. For AI-generated footage, use [Video Generation](/ai/video-generation) instead.

## Make a motion

1. Start a new chat and pick **Motion** under the message box, or ask for a teaser, an explainer, a walkthrough, or a social clip in any chat.
2. Describe what it should show, how long it should be, and who it's for. Attach a script or pictures to start from.
3. Send it.

Example:

```text
A 30-second product teaser for our note-taking app, with captions.
Show quick capture, search, and sharing, and end on the logo.
```

The Agent first lays the film out as a **Rough cut**: labeled boxes that stand in for each scene. Check the order and timing, then tell it to go on. To skip this step, say so in your request.

Where you didn't give a date, a price, or a name, the motion shows a placeholder instead of a made-up one. Tell the Agent the real value to fill it in.

Before it calls the motion done, the Agent plays every scene out of sight, catches scenes that fail or draw differently, and fixes each warning or tells you why it stays.

## What a motion can include

- Camera moves, a cursor that glides and clicks, hand-drawn marks, and a spotlight
- Caption chips, word-by-word text, and light effects
- Pictures and video clips
- Music the Agent writes, and narration in your [text-to-speech](/ai/text-to-speech) voice with captions

## Watch and review

- Click **Play**, or drag along the **Timeline** to scrub. **Previous frame** and **Next frame** step one frame at a time.
- Open **Scenes** to jump to a scene.
- Turn on **Loop**, click **Full screen**, or **Mute** the sound.
- Click **Turn off captions** to hide the narration's captions.
- Click **Comments** to leave a comment at the current moment. It shows as a marker on the timeline.

To change a scene, ask the Agent. It changes just the part you name.

The motion also plays in the iPhone and Android apps. See [Artifacts](/features/artifacts#open-artifacts-on-your-phone).

## Export

1. Click **Export**.
2. Choose **Download MP4**, **Download MP4 with captions**, or **Download GIF**.
3. Wait while it shows the progress. The file, up to 4 GB, goes into your Downloads folder.

The MP4 carries the music and narration. To share the motion with people or your Team, click **Share**. See [Artifacts](/features/artifacts#share-an-artifact).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The export is too large | Shorten the motion, or make it smaller or slower; for a GIF, download an MP4 instead |
| The motion asks for a date, price, or name | Tell the Agent the real value to replace the placeholder |
| **Scenes that failed** shows a count | Ask the Agent to fix the failed scenes |
| The motion shows boxes instead of drawings | It's still a rough cut; tell the Agent to go on |
