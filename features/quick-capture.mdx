Quick Capture saves the current app's content to a knowledge base for later. Each capture keeps a screenshot of the window and its text: for a browser, the page's content, and for a YouTube video, its subtitles. [Appshots](/features/appshots) attaches an app window to the question you're asking now; Quick Capture creates a knowledge base item you keep.

## Capture something worth keeping

1. Bring the app with the content to the front.
2. Press your Quick Capture hotkey, which you set under **Hotkey** in **Settings → Quick Capture**. Or open the [Dynamic Island](/features/dynamic-island) and click **Quick Capture**.
3. Enconvo saves the capture to the knowledge base set in **Save to**, the Default Knowledge Base unless you chose another. A short shutter sound plays.
4. Open that knowledge base and check the saved item.

Indexing and the summary can finish a little after the item appears.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Quick Capture**.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable Quick Capture** | Lets the hotkey save the current app. It also turns the [Voice Note](/features/voice-notes) hotkey on or off. On by default. |
| **Save to** | The knowledge base captures go to. Captures can't go to Memory. |
| **Capture sound** | Plays a short shutter sound when a capture is saved. |
| **Auto summary** | Summarizes each capture with AI. The summary shows in the item's preview and is searchable. |
| **Hotkey** | The shortcut that saves the current app. It can be a double tap of a modifier key, such as the right **Shift** key, or an ordinary shortcut. |

**Auto summary** sends the captured text to your AI model. Review your model and provider settings if you want to control where captured content is processed.

### X posts

The **X** section on the same page sets how the Enconvo [browser extension](/features/browser-extension) works on x.com.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Card style** | Shows saved posts as a **Post** or a **Screenshot**. Switching also changes posts you saved earlier. |
| **When you bookmark on X** | What X's own bookmark button does: **Bookmark on X**, **Bookmark and save to Enconvo**, or **Save to Enconvo only**. Until you choose, Enconvo asks on your first bookmark. Removing a bookmark keeps the post in Enconvo. |
| **Ask Enconvo button** | Shows the Enconvo button beside Grok in each post's header, to ask about the post and its replies. |
| **Auto summary for X posts** | Summarizes each saved post and its thread with AI. |
| **Auto categories for saved posts** | Tags each saved post with the topics it's about. Needs you to be signed in. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Content is missing or incomplete | Check [Permissions](/configuration/permissions), bring the intended app to the front, and try a small, clearly visible example. |
| You can't find the capture | Check **Save to**, then open that knowledge base. Indexing and the summary can finish after the item first appears. |
| The hotkey does nothing | Make sure **Enable Quick Capture** is on, and that a **Hotkey** is set. |
