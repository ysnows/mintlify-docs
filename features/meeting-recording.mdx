---
title: "Meeting Recording"
description: "Record a meeting or lecture, then review its transcript, summary, and follow-ups."
---

Recordings captures a meeting or lecture from your microphone, the call's audio, or both, and writes the transcript as people talk. Afterwards you can read a summary, name the speakers, and ask questions about what was said. The recording model is set separately from your Dictation and file Transcription models.

## Before you start

- Choose a transcription model in **Settings → Recording Models**.
- macOS asks for Microphone and system-audio access the first time you record them. Allow both for the sources you use.

<Note>
Make sure participants know about the recording, and follow the requirements that apply to your meeting.
</Note>

## Record a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new recording">
    Open **Settings → Recordings** and click **New recording**, or run **Start to Record (Meeting/Lecture)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the audio sources">
    Choose the **Microphone**, then what **Call audio** to record: **Off** records only your microphone, **All apps** records everything your Mac plays, and **One app** records only the meeting app's sound. Open the meeting app first to pick it. Check the **Transcription** model, and turn on **Live Translation** if the model offers it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click **Start recording**, and check that words appear in the live transcript.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Click **Stop** when you finish, then open the saved item under **Recordings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While you record, you can pause and resume, add notes, and press **⌘B** to bookmark a moment. The recording is saved as you go, so nothing is lost if Enconvo quits.

To change the sources later, click the arrow next to **New recording** and choose **Choose audio sources…**. In the same menu, **Meeting reminder** asks on the Dynamic Island whether to record when a Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or WeChat call starts.

## Review the result

Open a recording to see its **Transcript** beside **Summary** and **Ask AI**.

- **Transcript**: check who said what, especially where voices overlap. Click a speaker label to name that speaker; Enconvo then recognizes the voice in new recordings. Use the [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets) for recurring terms.
- **Summary**: an overview, the key points, decisions, and action items, written by AI from the transcript.
- **Ask AI**: ask about the recording. When you ask for follow-ups, ask for an owner and deadline only where the meeting stated one.

## When a step fails

Transcription, title generation, and search indexing are separate stages. An error in a later stage does not mean the audio or transcript is lost. Long recordings take a few minutes, and processing goes on if you leave the page.

Read the recording's processing status and use **Retry transcription** or **Retry index** on the step that failed. When you report a problem, **Copy details for support** copies the reference and log location support asks for, without the recording. Check it for private information before you send it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Audio is missing | Check the selected sources and macOS capture permissions. |
| The microphone is busy | Finish Dictation or Live Talk before you start recording. |
| **No transcription model is ready** | Choose a model in **Settings → Recording Models**. |
| A processing step failed | Retry that step from the recording's processing status. |
