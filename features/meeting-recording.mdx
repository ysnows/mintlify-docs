---
title: "Meeting Recording"
description: "Record a meeting or lecture, then review its transcript, summary, and follow-ups."
---

Recordings captures a meeting or lecture from your microphone, the call's audio, or both, and writes the transcript as people talk. Afterwards you can read a summary, name the speakers, and ask questions about what was said. The recording model is set separately from your Dictation and file Transcription models.

## Before you start

- Choose a transcription model in **Settings → Recording Models**.
- macOS asks for Microphone and system-audio access the first time you record them. Allow both for the sources you use.

<Note>
Make sure participants know about the recording, and follow the requirements that apply to your meeting.
</Note>

## Record a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new recording">
    Open **Settings → Recordings** and click **New recording**, or run **Start to Record (Meeting/Lecture)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the audio sources">
    Choose the **Microphone**, then what **Call audio** to record: **Off** records only your microphone, **All apps** records everything your Mac plays, and **One app** records only the meeting app's sound. Open the meeting app first to pick it. Check the **Transcription** model, and turn on **Live Translation** if the model offers it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click **Start recording**, and check that words appear in the live transcript.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Click **Stop** when you finish, then open the saved item under **Recordings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While you record, you can pause and resume, add notes, and press **⌘B** to bookmark a moment. The recording is saved as you go, so nothing is lost if Enconvo quits.

To change the sources later, click the arrow next to **New recording** and choose **Choose audio sources…**. In the same menu, **Meeting reminder** asks on the Dynamic Island whether to record when a Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or WeChat call starts.

## Review the result

Open a recording to see its **Transcript** beside a side panel with **Summary**, **Action items**, and **Notes** tabs, and **Ask AI**.

- **Transcript**: check who said what, especially where voices overlap. Click a speaker label to name that speaker; Enconvo then recognizes the voice in new recordings. Use the [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets) for recurring terms.
- **Summary**: the **Meeting summary** gives an overview, key points, decisions, figures, and a timeline whose times jump to that moment in the recording. With two or more speakers, **Talk time** shows each speaker's share of the conversation.
- **Action items**: the tasks found in the recording. Tick them off as you do them.
- **Notes**: the notes and bookmarks you added while recording. Turn on **Show translation** to add a translation in the recording's output language under each note; your notes stay as you wrote them.
- **Ask AI**: ask about the recording. When you ask for follow-ups, ask for an owner and deadline only where the meeting stated one.

Drag the side panel's left edge to widen it for long summaries, and double-click the edge to go back to the default width. The width is kept for every recording. You can select and copy text in every tab.

## Choose the summary language and template

At the top of a recording's side panel, pick the **Output language** its summary, action items, and template results are written in, and the template. The transcript and your own notes stay in the language they were in.

Besides **Meeting summary**, the built-in templates are **Lecture notes** and **Interview highlights**. Switch a recording between templates as often as you like; each template's result is kept.

To write your own template:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a template">
    Open the template menu at the top of the side panel and choose **New template…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it should pull out">
    Give it a **Name**, and describe in plain words what it should pull out of a recording. Pick a **Layout**: **Smart layout** can use cards, tables, checklists, flashcards, quizzes, and charts, while **Plain text** writes Markdown that is easy to copy elsewhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Click **Try it on this recording** to see what it gives. Nothing on the recording changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Turn on **Send tasks to Action items** if the tasks it finds should show in the **Action items** tab, then click **Save template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To set the output language and template every new recording takes, and to edit or delete your templates, click **Summary settings** next to **New recording** on the **Recordings** page.

## Review a call

Each Enconvo [Conference](/features/video-conference) call you record on your Mac gets its own entry in **Recordings**, even when calls use the same room. A call with notes on shows its transcript, who was there, its video, and its notes Doc.

The call's page opens with a **Meeting** overview:

- **Summary** and **Action items**, with who took each item and when it is due. Click **Add to Reminders** on an item to put it in Apple Reminders with its owner and due date.
- **Made on this call**: the docs and whiteboards agents made during the call.
- **On screen**: what agents saw on a shared screen.
- **Ask this meeting**: ask a question about the call.

Your Agent can also answer questions about your earlier calls in chat, and says which meeting each answer came from.

Calls your Agents make or take can land here too: turn on **Record calls** in **Settings → Agent Phone**, and each Agent call is saved to **Recordings** with its transcript. See [Agent Phone](/features/agent-phone#record-calls).

## When a step fails

Transcription, title generation, and search indexing are separate stages. An error in a later stage does not mean the audio or transcript is lost. Long recordings take a few minutes, and processing goes on if you leave the page. With Enconvo Cloud Fast, recordings up to 300 minutes transcribe from the whole file.

If live captions stop during a network drop, they reconnect, and the saved transcript still covers the whole recording.

Read the recording's processing status and use **Retry transcription** or **Retry index** on the step that failed. When you report a problem, **Copy details for support** copies the reference and log location support asks for, without the recording. Check it for private information before you send it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Audio is missing | Check the selected sources and macOS capture permissions. |
| The microphone is busy | Finish Dictation or Live Talk before you start recording. |
| **No transcription model is ready** | Choose a model in **Settings → Recording Models**. |
| A processing step failed | Retry that step from the recording's processing status. |
| The summary is in the wrong language | Pick the **Output language** at the top of the side panel. For new recordings, set it in **Summary settings**. |
| No action items | Use **Meeting summary**, or a template with **Send tasks to Action items** on. |
