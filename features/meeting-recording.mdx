title: "Meeting Recording"
description: "Record a meeting, review its transcript, and extract follow-ups."
---

Meeting recording captures a session for transcription and later review. Choose the recording model separately from your dictation or file-transcription model.

## Record a session

1. Open **Settings → Recording → Recording Models** and configure a provider.
2. Open **Recordings** and choose **Start Recording**, or run **Start to Record (Meeting/Lecture)**.
3. Review the recording panel's audio sources and controls. Grant microphone or system-audio access as required.
4. Start recording and confirm that the intended audio is being captured.
5. Stop when finished, then open the saved item under Recordings.

Make sure participants know about recording and follow the requirements applicable to your meeting.

## Review the result

Inspect the transcript and any available summary or chat view. Check who said what, especially when voices overlap. Use speaker naming controls to improve labels and [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets) for recurring terms.

Ask the Agent to separate decisions, open questions, and action items. Require an explicit owner and deadline only when they were actually stated in the meeting.

## Failure recovery

Read the saved item's processing status. Transcription, title generation, and search indexing are separate stages; a later-stage error does not necessarily mean the audio or transcript is lost. Use copyable diagnostic details when reporting an error, after checking for private information.

If audio is missing, check the selected source and macOS capture permissions. If the microphone is busy, finish Dictation or Live Voice before starting microphone capture.

## Related modes

Use [Transcription](/features/transcription) for an existing file, [Live Captioning](/features/live-captioning) for realtime subtitles, and [Voice Notes](/features/voice-notes) for a short spoken note saved into a knowledge base.
