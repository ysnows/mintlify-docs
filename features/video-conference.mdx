Video Conference lets you talk face to face with people and share your screen, right in Enconvo. Ring a friend, call your whole Team, or send a link that anyone can open in a browser. Captions can be translated into your language, and Notes records the call and writes a Notes Doc you can share with everyone on it.

This video covers Video Conference, then recording meetings in other apps, transcribing files, Live Captioning, Speakers, and Voice Note.

## Start a conference

Open **Video Conference** in the Chat Window sidebar, the Dynamic Island, or the menu bar menu. On a phone, start or join one from the iPhone or Android app (see [Calls and Friends on Your Phone](/integrations/phone-calls-and-friends)). On the Mac this opens the Video Conference Home:

- The box at the top takes a conference link to join, or a friend's or teammate's name to call. Type and press Return, or start a conference now.
- **Coming up** lists your scheduled conferences.
- **Earlier** lists the past month's calls with their recordings, and **Call again** rings the same people.
- **Call your Team** rings every teammate at once.

You can also open **Settings → Video Conference** and click **Start**.

## Invite people

In a call, open **People** and ring your friends, your teammates, or your own other devices. **Ring all** rings every teammate at once. A teammate who hasn't signed in to Enconvo shows under **Not on Enconvo yet**, with **Copy invite link**.

To invite anyone else, send the conference link at conference.enconvo.com. They can join from a web browser as a guest, talk, type in the chat, and share their screen from a computer.

Someone you know only from a call sees your name but not your email address until you become friends or teammates.

## Call a friend

Calling a friend from the Home box or from **Settings → Friends** (see [Friends and Friend Chat](/features/friends)) rings at once in the Conference window, with their face over your own blurred camera and a ringback tone. Once they answer, the call shows just their picture, and its **Chat** is your chat with them. Click **Add people** to turn it into a conference without a break.

An incoming ring has **Decline** and **Accept**. A call you miss leaves a card with **Message** and **Call back**. When you're already on a call, your other devices keep ringing and the caller hears that you're on another call. While you're on a call, your friends' messages show as small notices in the call instead of notifications.

## Join from a link

Opening a conference link, on the Mac or in a browser, shows a page before you go in:

1. Check your camera and microphone, and pick the devices to use.
2. Choose captions and the caption language, and a background.
3. Choose whether to go in muted. You also see who is already there.
4. Click to join.

In a conference with five or more people, your microphone starts off, with a note saying why. On a phone's browser, a guest who switches to another app shows as **Away**, and the call holds their place for up to 5 minutes.

## Schedule a conference

1. In **Settings → Video Conference**, click **Schedule conference**.
2. Choose **When it starts**, **How long**, and **How often**: once, every day, every weekday, every week, or every 2 weeks, all on one link.
3. Add people or an email, give it a title, and save.

The link stays the same, so people can save it now. Friends and teammates get an email with a calendar file; send the link to anyone else yourself. Change the time, length, title, or guests later and each person gets an update, or call the conference off. Scheduled conferences show under **Coming up**, and when one starts you get **Not now** and **Join**.

## In a call

The floating dock has separate **Mic**, **Camera**, and **Captions** buttons. One side panel holds **Chat**, **People**, and **Captions** tabs.

- **Layout**: the gallery keeps its shape as people join. Switch to speaker view, pin someone, or click someone to make them big. In a call with one other person, drag your own small picture to any corner or click it to swap.
- **Who is talking**: their tile gets a green ring and level bars.
- **Chat**: messages go to everyone, a count shows what you haven't read, and people who join later can read back. Attach, paste, or drop pictures and files up to 1 GB. Click anyone in the call to write to them alone.
- **Hands and reactions**: raised hands are listed in the order they went up, and reactions float over everyone's view.
- **Talking while muted**: hold Space to talk. If you talk while muted, a reminder offers **Unmute**.
- **Your video**: **Hide self view** or **Show self view**, **Mirror my video**, and **Stop incoming video**, which stops other people's cameras coming in to save data while voices and shared screens keep going. Choose your microphone, speaker, and camera during the call; on a phone you can switch to the back camera.
- **Backgrounds**: blur, scenes, patterns, or a picture of your own, chosen before you join or during the call. A mask blurs your whole picture if it loses your face, so nothing shows by accident.

Every button is reachable with Tab, menus move with the arrow keys, and Enter or Space makes someone big.

On a slow or unsteady connection, pictures pause before sound breaks up, and each person gets a picture size that fits their connection. A note tells you when your own connection is poor. A soft sound plays when someone joins or leaves a call of up to eight people. If you've been alone on a call for a while, **Still there?** asks before the call ends by itself.

## Share your screen

1. Click share in the dock. A tray shows a picture of each screen and window.
2. Pick one. Sound is shared too, from the Mac app and from a browser.

On the Mac, a green frame marks what you share, and a sharing bar has **Draw**, **Mute**, **Stop sound**, and **Stop sharing**. A small window shows whoever is talking without appearing in what you share. Enconvo's own pickers, alerts, and incoming call cards stay out of what others see, and sharing from a full-screen call steps out of full screen.

Others can open a shared screen full screen, and pinch to zoom on the iPhone. Anyone can draw on a shared screen from the Mac, the iPhone, or a browser: strokes land on the same spot for everyone, carry the drawer's name, and fade a few seconds later.

When you minimize the conference or share your screen, a small floating window shows who is talking, with mute, camera, leave, and a click back to the call.

## Captions

In a call, **Captions** turns them on or off. A band above the dock shows each line; with translation on, it shows the translation large and the original small, or the translation only, in the language you pick. A mark shows on a translation still coming.

Captions and translation cover every language the speech engine supports, sixty in all, listed by name in the language menus.

Click the live caption to scroll back through who said what and when, with the translation over the original words. Jump back to the latest line, or copy one line or all of them. This works on the Mac, iPhone, and Android.

## Notes and recording

Notes records the call, recognizes who is speaking, and writes AI notes into a Notes Doc. Everyone on the call sees and hears that notes are on. Each person turns their own notes on or off from **Notes** in the dock and gets their own Notes Doc.

With Notes on, the notes panel shows the transcript so far. Use **Ask AI** to summarize the call or answer questions about it, and add your own notes and bookmarks (**⌘B**), which go into the call's entry in [Recordings](/features/meeting-recording).

The notes list each action item with its owner and due date, the docs and whiteboards Agents made on the call, and what Agents saw on a shared screen.

The organizer can also click **Record conference** to save the call as a video file on their own computer. Everyone on the call, and anyone about to join, sees that it is being recorded.

## Organizer controls

The person who starts a conference is its organizer. They can:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Mute, **Ask to unmute**, **Ask all to unmute** | Mutes someone or everyone. Asking never turns anyone's microphone on for them. |
| **Spotlight for everyone** | Makes one person big for everyone. Anyone can step back to Gallery. |
| **Stop sharing** | Stops a person's shared screen. |
| Remove | Takes someone out of the conference. |
| **Lock conference** | New people wait in a waiting room until the organizer lets them in or turns them away. |
| **Guests ask to join** | Makes guests ask before they come in. |
| **Make organizer** | Gives someone the same controls. |
| **End for everyone** | Ends the conference for all. |

**End for everyone** and Remove start on **Cancel**. When no organizer is on the call, the person who has been there longest stands in as **Acting organizer**, so people at a locked door still get in.

## Agents in a conference

You can bring an Agent into a conference from **People**, or have your Agent join the conferences you choose. You can also send it into a Google Meet or Zoom meeting. See [Agents in Calls](/features/agents-in-calls).

## When a call ends

A wrap-up page shows how long the call lasted, who you talked with, what was saved, and **Rejoin** while the conference is still going.

If the Conference window stops working, it tells you the call stopped and offers **Rejoin** and **Close**. If your connection drops and doesn't come back within five minutes, you get **Rejoin**. Enconvo keeps the Mac's display on during a call.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Video Conference**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Join with microphone on**, **Join with camera on** | How you join a conference. You can still change it on the page before you join. |
| **Turn on captions when you join** | Shows captions as soon as you join. Only you see them. Each speaker's device writes their words while someone needs them. |
| **Translate captions** | Translates the captions into the language you pick. **Off** shows them as spoken. |
| **Take notes on my calls** | **Off**, **Notes**, or **Notes and recording**. Starts with each call you start, answer, or join. |
| **Record** | What Record starts as when you turn on Notes: **Audio**, or **Audio and video**. |
| **Recognize voices with Speakers** | Names the people talking from their voiceprint. New voices are added only when you choose. See [Dictionary and Speakers](/configuration/speech-assets). |
| **Share the Notes Doc with** | **People on the call** or **Only me**. You own the Doc and can change it later. |
| **Your agent at conferences** | Brings your Agent to the conferences you choose. See [Agents in Calls](/features/agents-in-calls). |
| **Mute or unmute from any app** | A shortcut, **⌃⌥M** by default, that works while you're on a conference, whichever app is in front. A short notice shows under the menu bar. Outside a call, the keys stay free for other apps. |
| **Do Not Disturb for calls** | Stops calls ringing this Mac for **1 hour**, **Until tomorrow**, or **Until I turn it off**. Messages still notify you, and you can still start and join calls. Also on the moon in **Settings → Friends**. |

## Meetings in other apps

For a Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or WeChat call, use [Meeting Recording](/features/meeting-recording): **Settings → Recordings → New recording** records your microphone, the call's audio, or both, with a live transcript. The arrow beside **New recording** has **Meeting reminder**, which asks on the Dynamic Island whether to record when one of those calls starts.

To have your Agent join a Google Meet or Zoom meeting for you, see [Agents in Calls](/features/agents-in-calls).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your camera or microphone isn't allowed | Click **Open System Settings** on the message and allow Enconvo, then try again. |
| Your microphone is off when you join | Conferences with five or more people start you muted. Click **Mic**, or hold Space to talk. |
| Someone can't get in | The conference is locked or guests must ask. Let them in from the waiting room. |
| Other people's video stops | Your connection is slow, or **Stop incoming video** is on. Voices and shared screens keep going. |
| Calls don't ring this Mac | Check **Do Not Disturb for calls** in **Settings → Video Conference**. |
| The call window stops working | Click **Rejoin** on the message in the window. |
