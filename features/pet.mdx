The Desktop Pet is a small animated character on your desktop. It mirrors what your Agents are doing, can show live session cards, and takes you to the session that needs attention. Click it to ask a question or open Enconvo. It's separate from an [Avatar](/features/avatar), which gives an Agent a character and a voice for conversations.

For other jobs, use [SmartBar](/features/smartbar) for a quick question or command, the [Chat Window](/ai/chat) for long conversations and files, the [App Sidebar](/features/app-sidebar) to work beside one app, [PopBar](/features/popbar) to act on selected text, or the [Dynamic Island](/features/dynamic-island) for a compact input at the top of the screen. [Window Management](/features/window-management) compares them all.

## Choose a Pet

1. Open **Settings → Pet**.
2. Choose a character in **My Pets**, or find one in **Gallery**.
3. Click **Wake Pet** to show it on your desktop.
4. Set **When tapped, open** to what clicking the Pet should bring up.
5. Turn **Show session cards** on or off, and adjust **Pet size**.
6. Go back to the desktop and click the Pet. What you chose in step 4 opens.

## Settings

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Wake Pet** / **Put Pet Away** | Shows or hides the Pet on your desktop. |
| **When tapped, open** | What clicking the Pet brings up: **Ask box** (the default), **Dynamic Island**, **Chat Window**, or **Smart Bar**. |
| **Answer with** | The Agent that answers what you ask the Pet. You can also change it in the Ask box. |
| **Session cards** | Live session status cards under the Pet. |
| **Pet size** | How large the Pet is. |

## Custom Pets

**Custom pets**, on the **My Pets** tab, shows the folder that holds your Pet packages. Click **Open folder**, add a Pet package in the supported format, then select the Pet in **My Pets**. A character image on its own isn't an animated Pet package.

To remove a Pet, click **Remove** on its card. This deletes the pack from your library; you can install it again from the Gallery.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Pet isn't on the desktop | Click **Wake Pet**, check that a Pet is selected in **My Pets**, and check **Pet size**. |
| Clicking the Pet opens the wrong window | Change **When tapped, open**. |
| You want voice conversations or a character for one Agent | Use an [Avatar](/features/avatar) instead. |
