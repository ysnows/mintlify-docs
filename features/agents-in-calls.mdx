Your Agent can sit in on a call with you. In an Enconvo conference it joins as a visible participant that anyone can turn on or ask by name, and it can make a doc or whiteboard the whole call edits together. In a Google Meet or Zoom meeting it joins as a guest, reads along, and answers your requests in the meeting's chat.

## Bring an Agent into a conference

1. Join the conference. You can bring an Agent only once you're on the call.
2. Open **People** and click **Add an agent**, then pick the Agent.
3. Choose how it answers and what it does when a guest joins (see [Options](#options)).

The Agent shows in the call as a participant, switched off. It listens only after someone turns it on or writes to it by name: type `@` in the chat to ask it. Then it answers and does work with your tools.

When someone other than you asks it for work, you get an approval card. **Always allow** lets that person ask for work for the rest of the call without asking you each time, **Just this once** allows this request, and **Not now** declines it.

The iPhone app works the same way: bring an Agent in from **People** with the same choices, and answer its approval cards from the phone.

## Work on the Stage together

When a conference Agent makes a doc or whiteboard during the call, it opens on the Stage, beside the call. Everyone signed in on the call can see it and edit it together while you talk. **Close the stage** hides it again.

## Bring your Agent to your conferences

Your Agent can join the conferences you choose on its own.

1. Open **Settings → Video Conference** and find **Your agent at conferences**.
2. Turn on **Bring … to conferences I set up**.
3. To include conferences you're invited to, turn on **Also conferences I'm invited to**. **Look in Calendar** finds Enconvo, Google Meet, and Zoom links in your Mac's Calendar events and skips the ones you declined. Click **Allow Calendar Access** if Enconvo asks.

The Agent joins once you're on, switched off. To bring it to one conference only, or to keep it out of one, use the **Bring** switch on that conference's row under **Coming up**. This Mac brings the Agent while Enconvo is open.

## Send your Agent into Google Meet or Zoom

Ask your Agent in a chat to join a meeting and give it the link, or turn on the **Bring** switch on a Google Meet or Zoom event under **Coming up**.

The Agent joins as “*Agent name* (Enconvo)” with its camera and microphone off. It reads the meeting's captions and chat and answers your requests in the meeting's chat. Before it does any work, it asks you on your Mac or iPhone. When the meeting ends, its notes are filed in [Recordings](/features/meeting-recording).

<Note>
The Agent joins through a browser on your Mac and needs Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or another Chromium browser. The host may have to let it in.
</Note>

## Options

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **How it answers** | **Written in the chat** (the default) or **Out loud**. |
| **When a guest joins** | **Leave the conference**, **Stay quiet until they leave**, or **Carry on**. |

Set the defaults for the Agents you bring automatically under **Your agent at conferences** in **Settings → Video Conference**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Agent doesn't answer in a conference | It starts switched off. Turn it on in **People**, or write `@` and its name in the chat. |
| The Agent shows **Waiting to be let in** | Ask the meeting's host to admit it. |
| **The meeting asks guests to sign in** | Click **Sign in its browser** and sign in once the meeting is over. |
| It reads only the chat in Google Meet or Zoom | The meeting's captions are off. Turn them on in the meeting. |
| The Agent didn't join a scheduled conference | Check the **Bring** switch under **Coming up**, and keep Enconvo open on this Mac. |
