A Space is a page of your own. You say what it should show, such as a tracker for your subscriptions or a board of deals gathered from several sites, and the Agent builds it and keeps it on the Chat Window sidebar's icon rail, below the line. To change it later, you ask.

## Make a Space

1. On the Chat Window sidebar's icon rail, click **Create a space** at the end of the rail. You can also choose **New space** in the rail's **···** menu, or click **New space** on the Plugins page's **Spaces** tab.
2. A new chat opens with `Create a Space that` already typed, and a dimmed suggestion after the cursor, such as tracking your subscriptions. Press **Tab** or **→** to take the suggestion, or finish the sentence yourself: what the page lists, what it totals, and what comes first. For example:

   ```text
   Create a Space that tracks my subscriptions. Call it Subscriptions. Netflix $15.49 renews Dec 3, Spotify $11.99 renews Nov 21, iCloud+ $2.99 renews Nov 28. Show the monthly total at the top and the next renewal first.
   ```

3. Send it. The Agent builds the Space, which takes a minute or so.
4. The Space appears on the rail, below the line. Click it to open it.

Drag Spaces on the rail to put them in the order you want. Your phones use the same order. To move a Space up among the places, open **Customize sidebar…** from the **···** menu. A Space that has its own navigation, such as Email, shows without a title bar, and the sidebar shows its name and navigation.

## What to ask for

Describe the page you want, and the Agent picks how to build it:

- **Pages you fill in**, such as a log, a tracker, or a daily score: the Agent builds it without asking where to import data from. A score shows how it was worked out, and a day with nothing logged says so.
- **Live values**, such as stock quotes: each value shows when its source set it and whether the market is closed, and rises and falls are colored the way your region does.
- **Dates and bills**, such as subscriptions: the Space can add a reminder to Apple Reminders before a renewal, and read receipts from mailboxes you let agents read.
- **Items from several sources**, such as deals from several forums: a source the Space can't reach is named at the top, and its earlier items stay.

On a phone, lists of numbers show each item on two short lines instead of a squeezed table.

## Change a Space

Open the Space and type what you want changed in the **Ask about** box on its page, then send it. For example: `Add Figma, $15 a month, renewing Dec 2.`

The Agent knows which Space you mean and where its files are, and makes the change. The open Space, and the Space beside the chat, update on their own a few seconds later. You can also ask about what the Space shows, and the Agent answers from it.

A Space you fill in yourself, such as a log or a tracker, saves every change you make on the page right away.

## Use the Space's menu

Right-click the Space on the rail.

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Plugin Settings** | Opens the Space's settings on the Plugins page. |
| **Share…** | Sends a link others can install, or lists the Space in the Plugin Store. See [Share a Space](#share-a-space). |
| **Remove from Sidebar** | Takes the Space off the rail. It stays in the **···** menu, where **Pin to Sidebar** puts it back. |
| **Customize sidebar…** | Picks what shows on the rail and in which order. |

## Share a Space

1. Right-click the Space on the rail and choose **Share…**. Sign in to your Enconvo account if Enconvo asks.
2. Under **Who can install**, choose **Anyone with the link** or **Plugin store**.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Copy link** and send the link.

Enconvo reviews each version before the link installs it. Everyone who installs the Space gets a copy of their own. Its data, your keys, and your sign-ins stay on your Mac.

If Enconvo's review declines a newer version, **Share…** shows why and offers **Fix in chat**.

To change a shared Space, open **Share…** again: **Update link** sends the newest version for review, and **Stop sharing** turns the link off. People who already installed the Space keep their copy.

## Find all your Spaces

The Plugins page has a **Spaces** tab that lists every Space you made. Its **New space** button starts a new one, the same as the sidebar's **Create a space**.

A new Space keeps separate data in each [Workspace](/configuration/account#workspaces), so using it in a client's Workspace doesn't show or overwrite your own entries.

## Spaces on your phone

The iPhone and Android apps show your Spaces in the sidebar, above Friends. Update the apps to get them.

- Tap a Space to open it full screen. Its **Ask about** box changes it, the same as on the Mac.
- Touch and hold a Space, then drag it to a new place. Your Mac keeps the new order.
- **Refresh** in the Space's menu gathers its newest items on your Mac, then shows it again.
- The Space's menu has **Share**, which sends its link through the phone's share sheet. For a Space you haven't shared yet, it opens **Share…** on your Mac.
- **New space** starts a new Space from the phone, with `Create a Space that` ready to finish.

A Space runs on your Mac. The phone shows it as you last saw it, then brings in the newest data from the Mac. The Spaces you opened last stay ready, so going back to one shows it at once. If your Mac is away, the sidebar shows the Spaces it last saw. Pair your phone first: see [iPhone App](/integrations/iphone-app).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Space doesn't show a change you asked for | Wait a few seconds for it to update. If it still doesn't, click the Space in the sidebar again. |
| A Space is missing from the sidebar | It was removed from the rail. Open the rail's **···** menu and click **Pin to Sidebar** next to the Space, or find it on the Plugins page's **Spaces** tab. |
| A Space that gathers from several sites shows a source at the top | The Agent couldn't reach that source. The Space keeps the items it got from it before. |
| **Share…** asks you to sign in | Sharing needs an Enconvo account. The link and the Space stay under your account. |
| **Share…** says the review declined a version | Read the reason, then click **Fix in chat** to have the Agent fix it, and update the link again. |
