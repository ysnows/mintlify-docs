A Space is a page of your own. You say what it should show, such as a tracker for your subscriptions or a board of deals gathered from several sites, and the Agent builds it and keeps it in the Chat Window sidebar under **Spaces**. To change it later, you ask.

The video below builds a subscriptions tracker as a Space, changes it by asking, and shows the Space's menu and the Plugins page's **Spaces** tab.

## Make a Space

1. In the Chat Window sidebar, click **New space** under **Spaces**.
2. A **Create plugin** chat opens with `Build a Space that` already typed. Finish the sentence: what the page lists, what it totals, and what comes first. For example:

   ```text
   Build a Space that tracks my subscriptions. Call it Subscriptions. Netflix $15.49 renews Dec 3, Spotify $11.99 renews Nov 21, iCloud+ $2.99 renews Nov 28. Show the monthly total at the top and the next renewal first.
   ```

3. Send it. The Agent builds the Space, which takes a minute or so.
4. The Space appears in the sidebar under **Spaces**. Click it to open it.

Drag Spaces in the sidebar to put them in the order you want. Your phones use the same order.

## Change a Space

Open the Space and type what you want changed in the **Ask about** box on its page, then send it. For example: `Add Figma, $15 a month, renewing Dec 2.`

The Agent knows which Space you mean and makes the change. The open Space updates on its own a few seconds later.

A Space you fill in yourself, such as a log or a tracker, saves every change you make on the page right away.

## Use the Space's menu

Point to the Space in the sidebar and click **⋯**, or right-click it.

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Plugin Settings** | Opens the Space's settings on the Plugins page. |
| **Share…** | Sends a link others can install, or lists the Space in the Plugin Store. See [Share a Space](#share-a-space). |
| **Keep in Dock** | Gives the Space its own Dock icon, which opens it in a window of its own. |
| **Show in Menu Bar** | Gives the Space its own menu bar icon, which drops the Space down from the menu bar. |
| **Remove from Sidebar** | Takes the Space out of the sidebar. Click **Undo** in the notice, or turn it back on later on the Plugins page. |

A Space in the Dock or the menu bar keeps its **Ask about** box, so you can change it from there too.

## Share a Space

1. Click **⋯** next to the Space and choose **Share…**. Sign in to your Enconvo account if Enconvo asks.
2. Under **Who can install**, choose **Anyone with the link** or **Plugin store**.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Copy link** and send the link.

Enconvo reviews each version before the link installs it. Everyone who installs the Space gets a copy of their own. Its data, your keys, and your sign-ins stay on your Mac.

To change a shared Space, open **Share…** again: **Update link** sends the newest version for review, and **Stop sharing** turns the link off. People who already installed the Space keep their copy.

## Find all your Spaces

The Plugins page has a **Spaces** tab that lists every Space you made. Its **New space** button starts a new one, the same as the sidebar's.

A new Space keeps separate data in each [Workspace](/configuration/account#workspaces), so using it in a client's Workspace doesn't show or overwrite your own entries.

## Spaces on your phone

The iPhone and Android apps show your Spaces in the sidebar, above Friends. Update the apps to get them.

- Tap a Space to open it full screen. Its **Ask about** box changes it, the same as on the Mac.
- Touch and hold a Space, then drag it to a new place. Your Mac keeps the new order.
- The Space's menu has **Share**, which sends its link through the phone's share sheet. For a Space you haven't shared yet, it opens **Share…** on your Mac.
- **New space** starts a new Space from the phone.

A Space runs on your Mac. The phone shows it as you last saw it, then brings in the newest data from the Mac. Pair your phone first: see [iPhone App](/integrations/iphone-app).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Space doesn't show a change you asked for | Wait a few seconds for it to update. If it still doesn't, click the Space in the sidebar again. |
| A Space is missing from the sidebar | It was removed from the sidebar. Find it on the Plugins page's **Spaces** tab, open it, and turn it back on there. |
| A Space that gathers from several sites shows a source at the top | The Agent couldn't reach that source. The Space keeps the items it got from it before. |
| **Share…** asks you to sign in | Sharing needs an Enconvo account. The link and the Space stay under your account. |
