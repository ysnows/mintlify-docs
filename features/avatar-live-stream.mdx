---
title: "Stream a Live Photo Character"
description: "Put a Live Photo character on an OBS stream with captions, and let her answer your Twitch, Kick, or YouTube live chat out loud."
---

A [Live Photo character](/features/avatar#create-a-live-photo-character) can host a live stream. Enconvo gives OBS a stage with her moving picture and a captions page, and she can read your stream's chat and answer viewers aloud through her Agent.

## Before you start

- Create a Live Photo character in **Settings → Avatar** and bind it to an Agent.
- Install OBS Studio. **Get OBS Studio** on the stream panel links to it.

<Note>
Atmee bills her live video by the minute while a call runs, whether or not you stream, and while she says answers in live mode.
</Note>

## Open the stream panel

1. Open **Settings → Avatar** and the **Live Photo** tab.
2. Click **Go live** on her card.

The stream panel shows her stage, her captions, the orientation (**Portrait 9:16** or **Landscape 16:9**), and OBS's stream state. Her stage shows her once her Avatar is open.

## Add her to OBS

1. In OBS, open **Tools → WebSocket Server Settings**, tick **Enable WebSocket Server**, and click **Apply**.
2. Click **Show Connect Info** and copy the server password.
3. On the stream panel, click **Connect OBS** and paste the password. Enconvo keeps it with your other credentials.
4. Click **Add to OBS**. Enconvo creates a scene named “Enconvo · *her name*” with her stage and captions, sized for your stream.
5. Start streaming in OBS, or with **Go live** on the panel. **End stream** stops it.

If you change the orientation, click **Add to OBS** again to resize her scene. To build the scene yourself, add the panel's transparent stage page and captions page to OBS as a **Browser** source.

## Choose her background

Under **Background** on the panel, choose what OBS gets behind her. OBS shows exactly what the stage preview shows.

| Choice | What OBS gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Transparent** | Only her, with alpha. Your OBS scene shows behind her. |
| **Color** | A full frame on one color, such as **Green screen** or a **Custom color**. |
| **Image** | A full frame on your own image, kept on this Mac. |
| **Blur photo** | A full frame on her photo, blurred. |
| **Original photo** | Her original photo. |

A character saved with a framed **Backdrop** keeps her own photo and frame; the background applies to cutout characters.

## Let her answer your live chat

1. On the stream panel, enter your **Twitch channel** or **Kick channel**, or connect YouTube with your YouTube Data API key.
2. Set who she answers: viewers who mention her, ask with `!ask`, or pay. Set per-viewer and overall cooldowns and blocked words, and turn on the approve-first queue if you want to approve each question.
3. Click **Start live chat**.

She answers through her Agent, out loud.

### Live mode

Turn on **Live mode** on the panel and she says each answer on her OBS stage with her lips moving, without a call. With it off, answers are read aloud on this Mac.

## Sound

During a call, while a stage shows her, her voice plays only from OBS. Turn on **Also play on this Mac** to hear her on the Mac as well.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The stage is empty | Open her Avatar. In a call, her stage shows her live. |
| OBS doesn't connect | Check that **Enable WebSocket Server** is ticked in OBS, and paste the password again. |
| Her scene is the wrong size | Click **Add to OBS** again after changing the orientation. |
| The background doesn't change | Her character was saved with a framed **Backdrop**. Backgrounds apply to cutout characters. |
| You hear her twice, or not at all | Check **Also play on this Mac**. |
