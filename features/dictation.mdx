Dictation turns what you say into text wherever you are typing, in any app. Voice Command sends what you say to your Agent, which answers out loud and can act for you. For a back-and-forth spoken conversation, use [Live Talk](/features/live-voice) instead.

The video tutorial below covers Dictation from start to finish in under 5 minutes: hold or tap the right Command key and talk, let it read names off your screen, teach it a name in the Dictionary, clean up or translate what you say, and choose the speech model, including one that runs on your Mac.

## Before you start

Enconvo needs Microphone permission to hear you. To type text into another app, it can also need Accessibility permission. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions).

## Dictate into an app

1. Click in a text field where you want the text, in any app.
2. Hold the right **Command** key and speak. A small pill shows that Dictation is listening.
3. Release the key when you finish. The text is typed in at the cursor.
4. Review the inserted text, especially names, dates, and numbers.

The right Command key, held while you talk, is the default. To tap once and speak as long as you need instead, open **Settings → Dictation → Dictation Hotkey** and change how you press it. You then finish with the hotkey again or the check mark on the pill.

Dictation puts a space before or after your words where they belong, except in Chinese, Japanese, and Thai. Say the name or keyword of one of your snippets on its own, and its text goes in instead of the words.

### Use the Dictation card

Point at the pill while you dictate and it grows into a card, with your words above its controls. From the card you can switch the microphone, turn the dictation into a **Draft**, pick a **Clean up** option, and turn **Return inserts and sends** and **Live transcript** on or off without stopping. After you stop, the card shows Clean up's progress.

To keep the pill small, turn off **Live transcript**. The pill then shows only its buttons and waveform.

### Send with Return

While you dictate, press **Return** to stop, insert the text, and send it in one press, such as a chat message. Stopping with the hotkey or the check mark only inserts the text. Turn this off with **Settings → Dictation → Insert → Return inserts and sends**.

To send every dictation without pressing anything, turn on **Send after inserting** in the same section.

### Pause and resume

When you tapped to start, you can pause: point at the level on the pill and click **Pause**, or press **⌥P**. The microphone goes off and your words stay. Click **Resume** or press the key again to go on, and finish with the hotkey or the check mark as usual. A dictation you hold a key for has no pause.

To change the key, open **Settings → Dictation → Pause / Resume**. It can be a key combination, a single key such as **Space**, or one modifier key that you tap, double-tap, or hold. Holding it pauses until you let go.

### Stay muted in your meeting

In a Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet call, a call in the Telegram app for Mac, or an Enconvo [Conference](/features/video-conference), starting a dictation mutes your microphone in the call, and finishing or pausing unmutes you. This works in the apps and in browser tabs. A call you muted yourself stays muted. When Enconvo can't find the call's mute button, it tells you once to mute yourself there. Turn this off with **Settings → Dictation → Mute my meeting microphone while dictating**.

## Write a draft by voice

Instead of dictating the words themselves, say what you want written, and Dictation writes a draft you review before anything goes in.

1. Click in a text field, such as a reply you are writing.
2. Hold the right **Command** key and **Shift** together, and say what the text should say, for example "Tell her Thursday at 2 works, and ask her to send the new screens the day before."
3. Let go. The draft appears in a panel above the Dictation pill as it is written.
4. Press **Return** to paste it, or **Esc** to discard it.

**Return** always pastes into the app the draft started in, and brings that app back first if another one is in front. The panel's header names that app.

You can also turn a dictation into a draft halfway: while dictating, tap **Shift** once, even after letting go of the Dictation key, and what you said so far becomes the draft's instruction.

With the draft still open, press the Dictation key alone and say what to change to refine it. Use Draft again in a text field to continue the text that is already there.

### Rewrite or answer selected text

Select text before you start a draft, and say how to change it. Draft shows its changes before it replaces the selection.

When the selection can't be edited, such as a message you received or part of a web page, Draft uses it as reference and writes new text, which **Return** pastes into the field you go back to. Turn this off with **Send selected text**.

With **Send app context** on, the draft is written from the app you are in, so it fits what you are working on: replying to an email, it can pick up details from the message you are answering.

These are in **Settings → Dictation → Draft**:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft modifier** | The key you hold with your Dictation hotkey to start a draft, or tap on its own while dictating. **Shift** by default. |
| **Show changes as a diff** | When a draft edits text, marks the words it added and removed. Off shows only the result. |
| **Send app context** | Gives the model the app's window title, web address, and visible text. On by default. |
| **Include a screenshot** | Also sends a picture of the app's window. Needs Screen Recording permission and a model that reads images. |
| **Send selected text** | Gives the model text you selected but can't edit, such as a message or a web page, as reference. On by default. |
| **Model for drafts** | The model that writes and edits each draft. |
| **Prompt for drafts** | How the model writes and edits each draft. |

## Use names on your screen

With **Settings → Dictation → Sense current app content** on, Dictation reads the app you are dictating into and spells names the way they appear there, such as a sender's name in the message you are replying to. The setting is on by default.

## Clean up or translate as you speak

Click the **Clean up** icon on the recording pill to choose what happens to your words before they are inserted:

- **Polish** removes filler words and false starts, and fixes typos and punctuation, in your own words.
- **Translate** types what you say in your Response Language, or in English if you already speak it.
- **On-Device Cleanup** cleans the text up with a model on your Mac, offline. It appears when your Mac can run it, and its model downloads once.

The **Clean up** chip shows the chosen option's icon; point at it to see its name. Click it again to move to the next option, or press **⌃⌥⌘I** (**Switch while dictating**). To pick the option that starts selected, or to add your own with a name, a prompt, and a model, open **Settings → Dictation** and use the **Clean up** section.

Translate and Fix Spelling & Grammar also remove ums, ahs, stutters, and false starts, so the result reads like written text. Their **Remove Filler Words** option is on by default.

When you switch Clean up in an app, the change applies to that dictation. Click **Always use …** on the card to keep that choice for the app, with **Undo**. In an app that already has a rule, the card offers **Make it the rule** and **Back to rule**.

### Choose a Translation Style

To change how **Translate** words its result, open **Settings → Dictation**, go to **Clean up**, and click the chevron on the **Translate** row. Then pick a **Style**:

- **Standard** is accurate and close to what you said.
- **Sound Native** rewords it freely, as if a native speaker wrote it.
- **Custom** follows a style you describe.

The style you pick shows as a badge on the **Translate** row. Text you say that is already in your target language is translated into your other language, whatever the style.

## Set rules for specific apps

**Settings → Dictation → App Rules** changes Dictation for one app or website: its **Clean up**, **Language**, and **Return key**. When a rule uses Translate, choose **Translate into** to translate into a different language in that app only, such as English in one chat app while Translate keeps your usual language everywhere else.

- A rule for Discord, Slack, Notion, Telegram, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude, Linear, Figma, or Teams also covers its website.
- The last row shows what every app without a rule uses, which comes from your Dictation settings.
- **Apps you dictate into often** suggests apps to add.

Dictating with the microphone in Enconvo's own message box follows Enconvo's app rule for Clean up and language.

<Note>
Polish, Translate, and options you add use your AI model, so the recognized text goes to that model's provider even when recognition ran on your Mac. On-Device Cleanup keeps it on your Mac.
</Note>

## Choose a speech model

1. Open **Settings → Dictation** and click **Change** next to **Speech Model**.
2. Pick from cloud models, models that run on your Mac, and models that use your own API key.
3. Click **Local** to see the on-device models. They work offline, and your voice stays on your Mac.
4. Select a model. A local model loads in a few seconds.

You can also open **Settings → Dictation Models** directly. Dictation, Voice Command, Live Talk, meeting recording, file transcription, and live captions each have their own speech model setting. A provider suited to batch files may not be the best choice for streaming input. See [Speech Recognition](/ai/speech-recognition) and the [provider catalog](/providers/catalog#speech-recognition).

## Teach it a name

A name that is not on your screen can still come out wrong, for example "Lumen" for a project called "Lumyn".

1. Open **Settings → Dictionary** and click **New word**.
2. Type the correct word, then what Dictation heard instead in **Heard as**.
3. Click **Save**, and dictate the name again.

See [Dictionary and Speakers](/configuration/speech-assets) for more about custom vocabulary and provider language and hot-word settings.

## Review past dictations

Open **Settings → Dictation History** to revisit past recognized text. From there you can search, copy, play the recording, transcribe a dictation again, and add a word to your Dictionary.

A dictation that couldn't be transcribed, took too long, or was cut off stays in the history with its recording. Click **Retry** to transcribe it again.

## Talk to your Agent with Voice Command

Voice Command is push-to-talk for your Agent. It answers on the Dynamic Island and out loud, does quick things such as opening an app at once, and hands bigger requests to your Agent as a task. It does not match a fixed set of phrases: it works out what you asked for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold the key and talk">
    Hold the right **Option** key and say what you need. Your words show on the Dynamic Island. You can also tap the key once to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Let go of the key, or tap it again if you tapped to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the answer">
    The answer shows under your words, and is read aloud while **Speak replies** is on. A task it starts runs on that Agent's own model and tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Press the key again to stop the answer, or press **Esc** to cancel while you are still talking. The Dynamic Island's microphone button works too.

Set it up in **Settings → Voice Command**:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hotkey** | The key you hold or tap. The right Option key by default. |
| **Listen** | The speech model that hears you (**Enconvo Cloud Fast** by default), plus the **Microphone** and **Language**, which follow Dictation unless you choose your own. |
| **Think** | The model that answers you. **Same as the Chat** (the default) uses Enconvo Fast when the chat runs on Enconvo Cloud, and a fast model of the chat's own provider otherwise, so on your own API key it doesn't spend Enconvo Cloud points. Tasks it starts run on the model picked for that chat. |
| **Answer** | **Speak replies** and the voice it answers in. With **Speak replies** off, answers only show on the Dynamic Island. |
| **Send a screenshot** | Sends the screen you're on with each request, so the Agent sees what you see. Enconvo windows are left out. |
| **Quick Actions** | **Manage** opens **Settings → Quick Actions**, which lists the things Voice Command and Live Talk do at once, and lets you turn them off or make your own. |

## Settings

These are in **Settings → Dictation**:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Dictation Hotkey** | The key that starts Dictation, and whether you hold it or tap it. Hold by default. |
| **Play sound effects** | Plays a short chime when Dictation starts and ends. |
| **Microphone** | The microphone Dictation records from. Click **Test** to check that it hears you. When it isn't connected, Dictation records from the one you used before. |
| **Dictation Language** | **Default** keeps the speech model's own setting; **Auto-detect** lets it recognize the language. |
| **Silence other audio while recording** | Pauses or mutes other apps while you dictate, and brings their audio back when you finish. **How to silence** picks pause, mute, or both. |
| **Mute my meeting microphone while dictating** | Mutes you in a meeting call while you dictate. On by default. |
| **Pause / Resume** | The key that pauses and resumes a dictation. **⌥P** by default. |
| **Sense current app content** | Reads the app you're in to spell names and terms, and gives Polish, Translate, and Draft context. |
| **Show live transcript** | Shows your words as you speak. Off leaves just the controls and the waveform. |
| **Return inserts and sends** | While you dictate, **Return** stops, inserts the text, and sends it. On by default. |
| **Send after inserting** | Sends the text when a dictation ends, without pressing Return. Off by default. |
| **Copy dictated text to the clipboard** | Also copies the recognized text. |
| **Insert snippets by name** | Say a snippet's name or keyword on its own, and its text goes in. |
| **When you switch apps while dictating** | Where the text goes when another app is in front by the time Dictation finishes. Asks by default. |
| **App Rules** | Changes any of these steps for specific apps or websites. |
| **Save dictation history** | Keeps each dictation's recording and text, for as long as **Keep history for** says. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No microphone input | When a notice names your microphone and says it sends no sound, pick another one or click **Test** next to **Microphone** in **Settings → Dictation**. Also check Microphone permission. |
| Text appears but is not inserted | Check Accessibility permission, click in the text field, and make sure the app accepts paste. |
| **Return** doesn't send | Check **Return inserts and sends** in **Settings → Dictation → Insert**, and the **Return key** column of any app rule for that app. |
| A dictation failed or was cut off | Open **Settings → Dictation History** and click **Retry** on it. |
| Wrong text after recognition | Check the **Clean up** option in use, **Dictation Language**, and your [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets). |
| Slow result after stopping | Batch models wait for the whole recording. Choose a model that streams. |
| Another feature owns the microphone | Finish Live Talk or a recording first. |
