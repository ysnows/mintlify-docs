Dictation turns speech into text in the app you are using. **Voice Trigger / Voice Command** sends a spoken request to an Agent. [Live Voice](/features/live-voice) is a separate two-way conversation feature.

The video tutorial below covers Dictation from start to finish in under 5 minutes: hold or tap the right Command key and talk, let it read names off your screen, teach it a name in the Dictionary, clean up or translate what you say, and choose the speech model, including one that runs on your Mac.

## Dictate into an app

1. Click in a text field where you want the text, in any app.
2. Hold the right **Command** key and speak.
3. Release the key when you finish. The text is typed in at the cursor.
4. For something longer, tap the right **Command** key once and speak as long as you need, then click the check mark on the recording pill.
5. Review the inserted text, especially names, dates, and numbers.

The right Command key is the default. To change the hotkey or gesture mode, open **Settings → Dictation**. Microphone permission is needed to capture audio. Inserting text into another app can require Accessibility permission.

## Use names on your screen

With **Settings → Dictation → Sense current app content** on, Dictation reads the app you are dictating into and spells names the way they appear there, such as a sender's name in the message you are replying to. The setting is on by default.

## Clean up, Polish, and Translate

Click the **Clean up** icon on the recording pill to choose what happens to your words:

- **Polish** removes filler words and false starts and keeps what you meant.
- **Translate** types what you say in another language, for example speak Chinese and get English.
- **On-Device Cleanup** cleans the text up with a model on your Mac.

Click the icon again to move to the next mode.

## Choose a speech model

1. Open **Settings → Dictation** and click **Change** next to the current model.
2. Pick from cloud models, models that run on your Mac, and models that use your own API key.
3. Click **Local** to see the on-device models. They work offline, and your voice stays on your Mac.
4. Select a model. A local model loads in a few seconds.

You can also configure providers under **Settings → Dictation → Dictation Models**.

## Post-actions

Dictation can run configured commands after recognition, such as **Dictation Polish** or **Dictation Translate** from Writing Toolkit. Review the post-action selection when the inserted text differs from the raw transcript. A post-action using a cloud model can send recognized text to that provider even if recognition itself was local.

## Send speech to an Agent

1. Open **Settings → Agents → Voice Trigger**.
2. Configure **Triggered Agent**, its hotkey, and **Session Timeout**.
3. Invoke the gesture and speak a complete instruction.
4. End recording and review the Agent's progress and result.

Voice Command can reuse the latest triggered Agent session within the configured timeout. It does not work by matching only a fixed set of phrases; the selected Agent interprets the request with its available tools.

## Teach it a name

A name that is not on your screen can still come out wrong, for example "Lumen" for a project called "Lumyn".

1. Open **Settings → Dictionary** and click **New word**.
2. Type the correct word, then what Dictation heard instead.
3. Click **Save**, and dictate the name again.

See [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets) for more about custom vocabulary and provider language and hot-word settings.

## History

Open **Dictation History** to revisit past recognized text.

## Choose recognition separately

Dictation, recording, file transcription, and live captions have separate model settings. A provider suited to batch files may not be the best choice for streaming input. The [Speech Recognition](/ai/speech-recognition) guide and [provider catalog](/providers/catalog#speech-recognition) explain those categories.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| No microphone input | Microphone permission and selected input device |
| Text appears but is not inserted | Accessibility, focused field, and whether the app accepts paste |
| Wrong text after recognition | Post-actions, language hints, and vocabulary |
| Slow result after stopping | Streaming versus batch model behavior |
| Agent receives the wrong conversation | Triggered Agent and Session Timeout |
| Another feature owns the microphone | Finish Live Voice or microphone recording first |

Use [Voice Notes](/features/voice-notes) to save speech directly into a knowledge base.
