Dictation turns what you say into text wherever you are typing, in any app. Voice Command sends what you say to your Agent, which answers out loud and can act for you. For a back-and-forth spoken conversation, use [Live Talk](/features/live-voice) instead.

The video tutorial below covers Dictation from start to finish in under 5 minutes: hold or tap the right Command key and talk, let it read names off your screen, teach it a name in the Dictionary, clean up or translate what you say, and choose the speech model, including one that runs on your Mac.

## Before you start

Enconvo needs Microphone permission to hear you. To type text into another app, it can also need Accessibility permission. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions).

## Dictate into an app

1. Click in a text field where you want the text, in any app.
2. Hold the right **Command** key and speak.
3. Release the key when you finish. The text is typed in at the cursor.
4. For something longer, tap the right **Command** key once and speak as long as you need, then click the check mark on the recording pill.
5. Review the inserted text, especially names, dates, and numbers.

The right Command key is the default. To change the key or how you press it, open **Settings → Dictation → Dictation Hotkey**.

## Use names on your screen

With **Settings → Dictation → Sense current app content** on, Dictation reads the app you are dictating into and spells names the way they appear there, such as a sender's name in the message you are replying to. The setting is on by default.

## Clean up or translate as you speak

Click the **Clean up** icon on the recording pill to choose what happens to your words before they are inserted:

- **Polish** removes filler words and false starts, and fixes typos and punctuation, in your own words.
- **Translate** types what you say in your Response Language, or in English if you already speak it.
- **On-Device Cleanup** cleans the text up with a model on your Mac, offline. It appears when your Mac can run it, and its model downloads once.

Click the icon again to move to the next option, or press **⌃⌥⌘I** (**Switch while dictating**). To pick the option that starts selected, or to add your own with a name, a prompt, and a model, open **Settings → Dictation** and use the **Clean up** section.

<Note>
Polish, Translate, and options you add use your AI model, so the recognized text goes to that model's provider even when recognition ran on your Mac. On-Device Cleanup keeps it on your Mac.
</Note>

## Choose a speech model

1. Open **Settings → Dictation** and click **Change** next to **Speech Model**.
2. Pick from cloud models, models that run on your Mac, and models that use your own API key.
3. Click **Local** to see the on-device models. They work offline, and your voice stays on your Mac.
4. Select a model. A local model loads in a few seconds.

You can also open **Settings → Dictation Models** directly. Dictation, Voice Command, Live Talk, meeting recording, file transcription, and live captions each have their own speech model setting. A provider suited to batch files may not be the best choice for streaming input. See [Speech Recognition](/ai/speech-recognition) and the [provider catalog](/providers/catalog#speech-recognition).

## Teach it a name

A name that is not on your screen can still come out wrong, for example "Lumen" for a project called "Lumyn".

1. Open **Settings → Dictionary** and click **New word**.
2. Type the correct word, then what Dictation heard instead in **Heard as**.
3. Click **Save**, and dictate the name again.

See [Dictionary and Speakers](/configuration/speech-assets) for more about custom vocabulary and provider language and hot-word settings.

## Review past dictations

Open **Settings → Dictation History** to revisit past recognized text. From there you can search, copy, play the recording, transcribe a dictation again, and add a word to your Dictionary.

## Talk to your Agent with Voice Command

Voice Command is push-to-talk for your Agent. It answers on the Dynamic Island and out loud, does quick things such as opening an app at once, and hands bigger requests to your Agent as a task. It does not match a fixed set of phrases: it works out what you asked for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold the key and talk">
    Hold the right **Option** key and say what you need. Your words show on the Dynamic Island. You can also tap the key once to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Let go of the key, or tap it again if you tapped to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the answer">
    The answer shows under your words, and is read aloud while **Speak replies** is on. A task it starts runs on that Agent's own model and tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Press the key again to stop the answer, or press **Esc** to cancel while you are still talking. The Dynamic Island's microphone button works too.

Set it up in **Settings → Voice Command**:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hotkey** | The key you hold or tap. The right Option key by default. |
| **Listen** | The speech model that hears you (Soniox by default), plus the **Microphone** and **Language**, which follow Dictation unless you choose your own. |
| **Think** | The model that answers you (Enconvo Fast by default). Tasks it starts run on their Agent's own model. |
| **Answer** | **Speak replies** and the voice it answers in. With **Speak replies** off, answers only show on the Dynamic Island. |
| **Send a screenshot** | Sends the screen you're on with each request, so the Agent sees what you see. Enconvo windows are left out. |
| **Quick Actions** | **Manage** opens **Settings → Quick Actions**, which lists the things Voice Command and Live Talk do at once, and lets you turn them off or make your own. |

## Settings

These are in **Settings → Dictation**:

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Dictation Hotkey** | The key that starts Dictation, and whether you hold it, tap it, or both. |
| **Play sound effects** | Plays a sound when Dictation starts and ends. |
| **Microphone** | The microphone Dictation records from. |
| **Dictation Language** | **Default** keeps the speech model's own setting; **Auto-detect** lets it recognize the language. |
| **Silence other audio while recording** | Pauses or mutes other apps while you dictate, and brings their audio back when you finish. |
| **Sense current app content** | Reads the app you're in to spell names and terms, and gives Polish and Translate context. |
| **Edit selected text by voice** | With text selected as you start, say how to change it and the selection is rewritten. |
| **Press Return after dictation** | Presses Return after the text is inserted. |
| **Copy dictated text to the clipboard** | Also copies the recognized text. |
| **When you switch apps while dictating** | Where the text goes when another app is in front by the time Dictation finishes. Asks by default. |
| **App Rules** | Changes any of these steps for specific apps or websites. |
| **Save dictation history** | Keeps each dictation's recording and text, for as long as **Keep history for** says. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No microphone input | Check Microphone permission and the microphone selected in **Settings → Dictation**. |
| Text appears but is not inserted | Check Accessibility permission, click in the text field, and make sure the app accepts paste. |
| Wrong text after recognition | Check the **Clean up** option in use, **Dictation Language**, and your [Dictionary](/configuration/speech-assets). |
| Slow result after stopping | Batch models wait for the whole recording. Choose a model that streams. |
| Another feature owns the microphone | Finish Live Talk or a recording first. |
