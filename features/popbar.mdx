PopBar is a small toolbar that appears next to text you select in any app. Its buttons can explain, translate, edit, search, copy, or read the selection aloud, and [PopBar extensions](#popbar-extensions) add more, such as a web search, a text converter, or sending the selection to another app. Use it when the text you want to work on is already on screen.

For other jobs, use [SmartBar](/features/smartbar) for a quick question or command, the [Chat Window](/ai/chat) for long conversations and files, the [App Sidebar](/features/app-sidebar) to work beside one app, or the [Dynamic Island](/features/dynamic-island) and [Desktop Pet](/features/pet) for a small entry point that stays on screen. [Window Management](/features/window-management) compares them all.

The video tutorial below sets PopBar up and uses it from start to finish in 6 minutes: turn it on, choose when it shows, run Fix Spelling, Translate and Ask AI, use Inline Mode, add and arrange buttons, make your own AI command, and get or build PopBar extensions.

## Use PopBar

1. Open **Settings → PopBar** and turn on **Enable PopBar**.
2. Select a short passage in an app.
3. Click a button on the toolbar.
4. Check the result before you use its copy, paste, or replace action.

You can also open PopBar with a shortcut: run **Show PopBar**, or set a **Hotkey** on the **When It Shows** tab. Press Escape to close the toolbar.

PopBar reads the selection through Accessibility permission, and what it gets depends on the app. Secure fields and some custom editors don't expose their text.

## Choose the buttons

The **Buttons** tab of PopBar settings shows the bar's buttons in one strip. Drag a button to reorder it, point to it and click **×** to take it off, or click it for more options. **Reset to Default** brings back the default buttons.

Buttons that don't fit on the bar are under **⌄** at its end. Click **⌄** to open the **More** panel: it lists those buttons, with a search field for every button and setting. Once you've changed the buttons, **Reset to Default** also appears there, under **Turn Off PopBar**, and brings back the default buttons and their order, with **Undo**.

Click **Add Buttons** to open the Button Gallery. It has Ask AI, Ask in Sidebar, Open Link, Search, Copy, Translate, Explain, Fix Spelling, AI Edit, Read Aloud, and more, the actions of your [PopBar extensions](#popbar-extensions), and a way to make your own [AI Command](/features/ai-commands). You can also add a [Writing Toolkit](/features/writing-tools) command for your own editing workflow. The toolbar shows the buttons you added.

## Control where it appears

The **When It Shows** tab sets when PopBar appears:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Which text** | Show PopBar for any text you select, or only for text it can help with, such as a foreign language, code, or an email. |
| **Where** | Keep it out of some apps or websites with **Everywhere except**, then **Add App** or **Add Website**. Or choose **Only in** so it shows only in the places you list. |
| **Show when I select text** | Turn this off to show PopBar only when you ask for it, then click **Add hotkey** and pick a shortcut. |

Leave out apps where an automatic toolbar would get in your way, such as Terminal.

## Change how it looks

On the **Look** tab, **Layout** puts the buttons in a row above the selection (**Horizontal**) or in a column beside the pointer (**Vertical**). If the bar covers what you're reading, move it with **Offset X** and **Offset Y** under **Fine-tune position**.

## Inline Mode

Some buttons can work without a result window. Right-click **Fix Spelling** on the PopBar and turn on **Inline Mode**. The next time you select text and click **Fix Spelling**, the fix lands right in your text.

## Change the result's model or settings

For commands with an AI model setting, the result toolbar offers a model picker. Changing it updates the command's default for later runs too. The result window's Settings button opens the command that produced the result, such as **Plugins → Writing Toolkit → Explain**.

## PopBar extensions

PopBar extensions are small add-ons whose actions become PopBar buttons. They use the extension format of [PopClip](https://www.popclip.app), so extensions written for PopClip install in Enconvo too. PopBar extensions need Enconvo for Mac.

<Note>
Enconvo isn't affiliated with Pilotmoon Software, the maker of PopClip.
</Note>

### Install from Enconvo's catalog

1. Open **Plugins** and choose the **PopBar Extensions** tab.
2. Browse the catalog by category, or type in the search field to find one.
3. Click **+** next to an extension, then **Install**.
4. The card that follows lists the extension's buttons, when each one shows, and where it is. Choose **On Bar**, **In ⌄ More**, or **Off** for each, then click **Done**. **Settings…** opens the extension's options.

New buttons go to the front of the PopBar's **⌄ More** panel, or onto the bar while it has room. **⌄** shows a dot until you open the panel, where the new buttons are marked **NEW**.

Enconvo reviews every extension in its catalog before listing it, and installs exactly the version it reviewed. Installed extensions are listed at the top of the tab. When Enconvo has reviewed a newer version of one, its row in the catalog shows **Update**. In the Button Gallery, **Get more…** opens the same tab.

You can also browse the catalog on the web at [app.enconvo.com/popbar-extensions](https://app.enconvo.com/popbar-extensions). On your Mac, an extension's **Install** button opens Enconvo, which shows what the extension can do before anything installs.

### Add an extension from elsewhere

Click **Add PopBar extension…** on the **PopBar Extensions** tab, or in the **Add** menu of the Plugins page. Then do one of these:

- Paste a link to a `.popclipextz` package.
- Paste a snippet, the short text form of an extension that starts with `#popclip`.
- Click **Choose file…** to pick a `.popclipextz` package or a `.popclipext` folder on your Mac.

Click **Preview**. Enconvo reads the extension without running it and lists what it can do beyond the PopBar, such as running scripts, connecting to the internet, opening apps, or pressing keys. Nothing installs until you click **Allow and install**, which ends on the same card of buttons as an install from the catalog. Enconvo doesn't review extensions from outside its catalog, so install them only from a source you trust.

When you select a snippet in any app, the PopBar shows an **Install Extension** button first. Clicking it opens the same preview.

### Import from PopClip

If you use PopClip, click **Import from PopClip…** on the **PopBar Extensions** tab. Enconvo copies your PopClip extensions and their settings, in PopClip's order, and adds their actions to the PopBar. PopClip itself isn't changed.

- An extension that is turned off in PopClip installs, but isn't added to the PopBar.
- API keys and passwords aren't copied. Enconvo lists the extensions that need them, so enter them again in each extension's settings.
- An extension that matches a version in Enconvo's catalog counts as reviewed and updates from the catalog.

### Settings, updates, and removal

Open an extension from the **PopBar Extensions** tab to see its page. Its **PopBar buttons** section lists each button and when it shows, and lets you put it on the bar, in **⌄ More**, or off the PopBar. **Arrange all buttons in PopBar Settings** opens the Buttons tab.

An extension with options shows **Settings** at the top of its page, and **Settings…** on its card in the Button Gallery.

An extension installed from a link updates from the same link. When an update can do more than the version you allowed, Enconvo shows only what's new and asks again before updating.

To remove an extension, open it from the **PopBar Extensions** tab and uninstall it. Its buttons leave the PopBar.

### What isn't supported yet

- Extensions that sign in to an account.
- Extensions that build their actions each time the bar appears.

Enconvo tells you during the preview when an extension uses one of these.

### Suggest an extension

Enconvo's catalog lives in [github.com/Enconvo/popbar-extensions](https://github.com/Enconvo/popbar-extensions). Open an issue or pull request there to suggest an extension for review.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No toolbar appears | Check **Enable PopBar**, Accessibility permission, and the apps and websites under **Where**. |
| It acts on the wrong text | Make a fresh selection and open PopBar again. |
| A command is missing | Check the **Buttons** tab, and check that the command's plugin is installed. |
| The result doesn't replace your text | The app may not allow it. Copy the result and paste it into an editable field. |
| Extension buttons don't appear | Click **⌄** at the end of the PopBar, where new buttons go, or check the extension's page in Plugins: an **Off** button isn't on the PopBar. Some buttons show only for text they can work on: **Base64 Decode**, for example, shows only for text that looks like Base64, so selecting plain text shows only **Base64 Encode**. |
| An extension button replaced the selected text | Many extensions paste their result over the selection, as they do in PopClip. Where the text can't be edited, the result is copied instead. |
| The catalog doesn't load | Check your connection, then click **Try again** on the **PopBar Extensions** tab. |
