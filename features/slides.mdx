Ask an Agent for a deck and it writes the slides in a panel beside the chat. You present it from there, edit it by hand, and download it as a PowerPoint with real text boxes, shapes, tables, speaker notes, and sections, or as a PDF. Use it whenever you need slides to present or hand over.

## Make a deck

1. Start a new chat and pick **Slides** under the message box, or just ask for a deck in any chat.
2. Describe the talk: who it's for, how long, and what it should cover. Attach a PDF, notes, or a `.pptx` to start from.
3. Send it. Slides appear in the panel as the Agent writes them; a longer deck is written several slides at a time, side by side.

Example:

```text
A 5-minute talk from this doc, with speaker notes.
Keep one idea per slide and end with three next steps.
```

When the Agent finishes, it checks the layout. The header shows **Slides that fit** or **Problems on the slides** with a count. Click the problems to see them: text off the slide, cut off, or overlapping, low contrast, small text, and broken pictures. Ask the Agent to fix them.

## Present

- Click **Present** to play the deck full screen with its builds and transitions.
- Open **More ways to present** next to it and choose **Present from this slide**, or **Presenter view** to see the time, the next slide, and your notes.
- With a second display connected, Presenter view shows the audience window there.

You can also open and present the deck in the iPhone and Android apps. See [Artifacts](/features/artifacts#open-artifacts-on-your-phone).

## Edit the deck

You can change the deck yourself or ask the Agent. When you ask, it changes just the slides you name, and the panel updates them in place while keeping the slide you're on.

- **Words**: double-click text to retype it. Use **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Text color**, and **Clear formatting** on the selection.
- **Layout**: drag an element to move it.
- **Speaker notes**: click **Speaker notes** and type under the slide.
- **Slides**: open **More** for **Duplicate slide** (which takes you to the copy), **Move slide earlier**, **Move slide later**, and **Delete slide**. **Undo** brings a deleted slide back.
- **Comments**: click **Comments**, then comment on a spot, on selected words, or on the whole slide. Start a comment with `@Enconvo` to ask the Agent.

## Use a design system

A design system gives every slide the same colors and typefaces, and they go into the PowerPoint's theme too.

1. Open **More → Design system** and pick a system.
2. Ask in the chat to restyle the slides with it. The words stay as they are.

When the system has changed since the deck used it, the menu says it is behind its source and offers **Sync with its source**. To make a design system from your own brand, see [Design Canvases](/features/design#use-a-design-system).

The Agent can also place icons from a set of about 1,500 on a slide.

## Download

Click **Download** and choose **Download PowerPoint** or **Download PDF**. Enconvo saves the file and shows where.

To share the deck with people or your Team, click **Share**. See [Artifacts](/features/artifacts#share-an-artifact).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Problems on the slides** shows a count | Click it to see each problem, then ask the Agent to fix them |
| A change you typed wasn't kept | The slide changed while you edited it; make the change again |
| A slide you deleted didn't come back | The deck changed since; open an earlier version from the version menu |
| Export fails with "hasn't finished drawing the export" | Update Enconvo, then download again |
