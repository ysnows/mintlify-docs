An artifact is something an Agent makes for you to keep: a doc, a slide deck, a design canvas, a dashboard, a motion, a web page, a diagram, or code. It opens in a panel beside the chat, keeps every version, and stays in the **Artifacts** library after the chat ends. Reach for one when you'll come back to the result, edit it, present it, or share it.

Images, videos, and audio you generate live in [Media](/features/media) instead.

## Start an artifact from a new chat

A new chat shows a **Make something** row under the message box, with a pill for each kind: **Slides**, **Doc**, **Design**, **Motion**, **Dashboard**, **Web page**, **Image**, and **Video**. Each pill has a short caption, so you can tell **Motion** (animated with code) from **Video** (AI footage).

1. Click a pill. Nothing is sent yet: the message box switches to that kind.
2. Choose how to start:
   - Click one of the three examples to fill the message box.
   - Click **Blank deck**, **Blank doc**, **Blank design**, and so on, to open an empty one beside the chat.
   - Click **Open a .pptx**, **Open a .docx**, or **Open a spreadsheet** to start from a file.
   - Click **Your decks**, **Your docs**, and so on, to open the ones you already have.
3. Describe what you want and send it.

The artifact opens beside the chat and fills in as the Agent works. You can also just ask in any chat: a request for a teaser, an explainer, or a walkthrough video makes a [Motion](/features/motion), and a request for figures to track makes a [Dashboard](/features/dashboards).

**Image** and **Video** show your recent pictures or clips and an **Open Media** link instead. See [Media](/features/media).

## Find your artifacts

Click **Artifacts** in the Chat Window sidebar. The library opens on a grid of thumbnails, newest change first.

- Switch between **Grid view** and **List view** at the top.
- Filter by kind: **All**, **Pages**, **Designs**, **Dashboards**, **Slides**, **Motion**, **Documents**, **Diagrams**, or **Code**.
- Type in **Search artifacts** to find one by its title, in any alphabet and either case.
- Click **New** to start a blank one of any kind.
- Click **Import** to bring in a document as a doc, or a design's or dashboard's zipped project folder.

Right-click an item, or click its **⋯**, to **Open** it, **Rename…** it, **Copy link**, **Open the chat it was made in**, **Select** several, or **Delete** it. To pick several at once, ⌘-click or ⇧-click them, then delete them together. Deleting removes every version and everything the artifact saved; the chat it was made in stays. Click **Undo** in the notice that follows to bring it back.

A blank artifact you never write into isn't kept: it's thrown away when you close it.

## Go back to an earlier version

Every change the Agent makes, and every edit you save, becomes a new version.

1. Click the version number (for example **v4**) in the artifact's header.
2. Under **Versions**, pick the one you want.
3. Click **Restore**, or **Back to the current version** to leave it as it is. A restore is saved as a new version, so nothing is lost.

You can also ask in the chat: "Bring the deck back to how it was before the color change." The Agent restores that version of the design, deck, or dashboard and keeps the rest.

When you ask for a change, the Agent edits just the part you name, such as one slide, one chart, or one artboard, and leaves the rest as it was.

## Share an artifact

Docs, designs, decks, dashboards, and motions can be shared with people or your Team. You need to be signed in to your Enconvo account.

1. Open the artifact and click **Share**.
2. Type an email under **Add people by email**, pick **Can view**, **Can comment**, or **Can edit**, and click **Share**.
3. To share with your Team, set **Team access**. To let anyone signed in to Enconvo open it, set **General access** to **Anyone with the link**, then click **Copy link**.
4. Click **Done**.

Sharing moves the artifact to Enconvo Cloud, so the people you add open it on their own Mac and see each new version. Comments that start with `@Enconvo` get an answer from Enconvo.

## Publish a page as a website

A web page artifact can go online at its own address, `name.site.enconvo.com`, on Enconvo Cloud. Publishing is included with your Enconvo account.

1. Open the page and click **Publish** in its header.
2. Under **Site name**, type the name for its address: 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits, or hyphens.
3. Under **Who can see it**, choose **Anyone with the link**, or **Password** and type a password of at least 4 characters.
4. Click **Publish**. When it says **Your site is live**, click **Open** to see it.

The button then reads **Published**. When you change the page, it reads **Update**: click it, then **Update**, to put the newest version online. In the same panel you can **Copy link**, **Put back** an earlier published version, or **Unpublish** to take the site offline, which stops the link working for everyone.

You can also ask an Agent to share a page online, and it publishes the page or a folder of a site for you.

## Open artifacts on your phone

Decks, dashboards, and motions also open in the iPhone and Android apps. Open one from a chat or from the **Artifacts** library, then comment, ask `@Enconvo`, share, export, or present it.

## Use a doc as a skill

A doc can serve as a skill your Agent follows. Open the doc and choose **Use as a skill…**. When the doc belongs to someone else, only you can turn it on, and Enconvo notes that its owner can still change what your Agent follows.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An image or video isn't in the library | Images, videos, and audio live in [Media](/features/media) |
| **Share** asks you to sign in | Sign in to Enconvo on your Mac, then share again |
| A shared doc no longer opens | Its owner removed your access, or you took it off your Library |
| The Agent rewrote more than you asked | Name the part to change, or restore the version before it |
| A published page shows an old version | Click **Update** in the page's header, then **Update** |
