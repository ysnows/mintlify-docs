When an agent works in a browser tab or an app window you can't see, a small Picture-in-Picture window in the corner of the screen shows it live. You keep working in your own apps, glance at what the agent does, and step in the moment it needs you.

It works with [Browser Use](/features/browser-control) and [Computer Use](/features/computer-use) on the Mac.

## Watch the agent work

Start a task that uses Browser Use or Computer Use. While the agent works out of sight, the live view shows in the corner of the screen. Tabs refresh about three times a second, and app windows stream smoothly.

When several tasks wait at once, their cards stack, and **Show all** says how many there are. Open the stack and a bar across the top says how many are running, with **Collapse** to fold the cards back into one and **Close** to hide the window. Each card shows its app's icon with a dot for the task's state. Drag the window and its cards move together.

A page with another extension's widget that can't be shown says it has no live preview.

## Step in when the agent needs you

When the agent asks for you, its window turns amber and says what it needs.

1. Click the Picture-in-Picture window. Enconvo takes you to the tab or app window.
2. Do what the agent needs.
3. Click **Return to agent** to hand it back. The agent goes on from there.

You can click the window to take over at any time, not only when it asks.

## Settings

Choose when the window shows, separately for each:

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings → Browser Use → Picture-in-Picture Window** | **Always** (default), **Only When I Am Needed**, **Off** |
| **Settings → Computer Use → Picture-in-Picture Window** | **Always** (default), **Only When I Am Needed**, **Off** |

**Only When I Am Needed** keeps the window hidden until the agent asks for you.
