title: "Friends and Friend Chat"
description: "Add friends by email or QR code, message them and your Team, send files and voice messages, start group chats, and call them straight away."
---

Friends are the people you message and call from Enconvo. Add someone by email or QR code, then chat with them, send files, voice messages, AI answers, and Artifacts, or start a call in one click. Your Team members show beside your friends, so you can reach them the same way.

## Before you start

Sign in to your Enconvo account on **Settings → Account**. Friends and Friend Chat need it, and the people you add need their own Enconvo account.

## Add a friend

1. In the Chat Window sidebar, find **Friends** above your Projects and click **+**, then choose **Add a friend…**.
2. Type their email under **Add by email** and press Return. Or let them scan **Your QR code**, or click **Copy link** and send them the link.
3. They get a request. Once they accept, they show under **Friends** in the sidebar.

Requests sent to you show in the sidebar with a count. Click **Accept request** or **Not now**. **New code** replaces your QR code and link, and the old ones stop working.

## Manage friends

Point to **Friends** in the sidebar and click **Manage friends**, the gear beside **+**, to open the Friends page. **Settings → Friends** opens it too, and so do **Friends** in the menu bar menu and the **Friends** command in SmartBar.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Your friends, with **Message** and **Call**. |
| **Requests** | Requests waiting for your answer, and the ones you sent. |
| **Groups** | Your group chats. |
| **Team** | Your Team members. |
| **Blocked** | People you blocked, with **Unblock**. |

A friend's **⋯** menu has **Message**, **Call**, **Send a file…**, **Schedule a conference**, **Mute chat**, **Keep in the sidebar**, **Remove friend**, and **Block person**.

If you're in a Team with Client Workspaces, each teammate shows in the Friends list of the Workspaces you share with them. See [Workspaces](/configuration/account#workspaces).

## Chat with a friend

Click a friend in the sidebar to open your chat. Replies show as they arrive, and you get a notification when the chat isn't open. The Enconvo Dock icon shows how many friend messages you haven't read. It counts chats read on any of your devices and leaves out muted chats.

In the message box you can:

- **Send files**: drop, paste, or pick files up to 1 GB each. A file keeps sending when you leave the chat, and picks up where it stopped after a dropped connection.
- **Record a voice message**: click the voice button and talk, up to a minute. Send it as a voice message, or choose **Send as text** to turn it into text you can edit first. A voice message plays in the chat, and **Show text** reads it.
- **Add emoji**: click **Emoji** to search for one. It uses the skin tone you picked in the Mac's Emoji Picker.

A video shows its first frame and length and plays in its bubble. A PDF or text file opens with one click. **Save As…** keeps a copy of any file.

### Reply and react

Point to a message, or right-click it, to react with a quick emoji or any emoji, or to **Reply** with a quote. Click a reaction to add or remove yours, and click a quote to jump to the original message. The **Chat** tab of a friend call shows the same quotes and reactions, and you can react and reply there too.

### Select several messages

1. Choose **Select** from a message's menu, or **Select messages** from the chat's menu.
2. Click messages to pick them. Shift-click picks a range.
3. **Copy**, **Forward**, or **Delete** them together, or click **New chat** to start an agent chat with them. Pick the Project, tell the agent what to do with the messages, and click **Create chat**.

## Start a group chat

1. Click **+** next to **Friends** in the sidebar and choose **New group…**. You can also click **New group** on the Friends page, or **Add people** in a chat's details.
2. Choose who is in the group, and give it a **Group name** and picture if you want.
3. Click **Create group**.

In a group, type `@` to mention someone. **Seen by** shows who has seen your message. Open the group's details to rename it, change its picture, set its **Notifications** (**All**, **Mentions**, or **Off**), add or remove people, **Make owner** to hand it on, or **Leave group**.

## Send AI answers and Artifacts to friends

Click **Send to a friend** under an AI answer or at the top of an [Artifact](/features/artifacts). Pick one or more friends, add a note if you want, and click **Send**. An Artifact arrives as a read-only copy.

A friend's answer or Artifact never loads pictures from the web or moves itself to a web page.

## Call a friend

Click **Call** next to a friend in the sidebar, on the Friends page, or in your chat. The call opens in [Video Conference](/features/video-conference). **Schedule a conference** in a friend's menu sets one for later.

## Let an agent message your friends

Ask Mavis or any agent to message, call, or send files to a friend, read a Friend Chat, answer a friend request, or add, remove, block, mute, or pin someone. To name someone in a chat, type `@` and pick them: the agent knows whom you mean. Below Full access, the agent asks before it reads your chats or contacts a friend, and the approval shows who it's for and what it sends. At Full access it goes ahead.

When an agent calls a friend, it rings their Enconvo apps, talks with them for you, and brings their answer and the call's transcript back to your chat. Agents reach only your friends and teammates. See [Agent Phone](/features/agent-phone#have-an-agent-call-a-friend).

## Report or block someone

Choose **Report…** in the chat's menu, or right-click someone else's message and choose **Report…**. Write your reason and click **Send report**. The Enconvo team reviews it, and Enconvo offers to block the person.

To block someone without reporting, choose **Block person** in their menu. Unblock them on the Friends page's **Blocked** tab.

## Your profile photo

Your Google photo shows by default. To change it, open **Settings → Account** and click **Change photo**: **Upload photo…** lets you pick and crop your own, **Use Google photo** goes back to it, and **Remove photo** removes it.

## Friends on your phone

The iPhone and Android apps have Friend Chat too, with files, pictures, and calls: see [Calls and Friends on Your Phone](/integrations/phone-calls-and-friends). Group chats, reactions, and voice messages work there as well:

- Tap **+** in the Friends list to start a group, with `@` mentions, **Seen by**, and the same group details as on the Mac.
- Hold a message to react or reply. Tap a reaction to add or remove yours, and tap a quote to jump to the original. On iPhone, hold a reaction to see who reacted.
- Hold **Hold to Talk** to record a voice message of up to a minute, and let go to send it. Slide to **Cancel** to throw it away, or to **Text** to edit it as text first.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Friends** shows **Sign in to add friends** | Sign in on **Settings → Account**. |
| Someone's QR code doesn't work | They made a new code. Ask them for their new QR code or link. |
| A file won't send | Each file can be up to 1 GB. Larger files are refused. |
| A message shows **Not sent** | Click **Try again**. Messages also send by themselves when the connection returns. |
| You don't get notifications from a chat | The chat is muted. Choose **Unmute chat** in its menu, or check the group's **Notifications**. |
