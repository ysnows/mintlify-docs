Enconvo runs on macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Some on-device features, such as [Local LLM (MLX)](/providers/mlx), need Apple Silicon, and local models also need enough memory and disk space.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download">
    Get the DMG for your Mac's processor from [enconvo.com](https://www.enconvo.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it to Applications">
    Open the DMG and drag **Enconvo** into **Applications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Enconvo">
    Open the copy in **Applications** and follow the welcome steps. Then continue with the [Quick Start](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep only the copy in **Applications**. Shortcuts, permissions, and updates all point to one copy, and a second copy elsewhere can get permissions that the one you use doesn't have.

## Update

Choose **Check for Updates** from the Enconvo menu bar icon. To get new features sooner, open **Settings → General** and set **Update Channel** to **Beta** or **Dev**; **Production** is the default. See [Updates](/configuration/updates).

The app and its built-in plugins update separately. If a feature is missing or behaves differently, check for both.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The app doesn't open | Check that your Mac runs macOS 14 or later and that the download finished. |
| A permission is on but the feature still can't use it | Quit Enconvo and open it again from **Applications**. Make sure no older copy is running. |
| A local model won't download | Check disk space and your connection, and that the model supports your Mac. |
| You can't find the app window | Enconvo lives in the menu bar. Click its icon, or press **⇧⌘D** for SmartBar. |
| macOS says the download is damaged or can't be checked | Download a fresh copy from [enconvo.com](https://www.enconvo.com). Don't turn off macOS security settings. |
