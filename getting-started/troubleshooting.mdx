Find your problem below and try the fix in the right column. Most problems come down to a provider that isn't connected, a missing macOS permission, or an old copy of the app. If nothing here helps, [send diagnostics](#send-diagnostics) and ask on Discord.

## Try these first

1. **Quit and reopen Enconvo.** Choose **Quit** from the Enconvo menu bar icon, then open Enconvo from **Applications**.
2. **Update.** Choose **Check for Updates** from the menu bar icon.
3. **Check the model.** Open **Settings → AI Model** and make sure the provider you use is connected. If you're not sure, sign in to your Enconvo account and use [Auto Route](/providers/auto-route).
4. **Check permissions.** Open **Settings → Permissions** and turn on what the feature needs. See [Permissions](/configuration/permissions).

## Answers and models

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No answer, or an error about the provider | Open the provider in **Settings → AI Model** and click **Validate**. For a plan sign-in, open the account card's menu and click **Reconnect**. |
| An error about limits, quota, or points | Check the provider's usage page, or **Settings → Points Usage** for Enconvo Cloud. Switch to another provider for now. |
| Every cloud provider fails | Check your internet connection. Behind a proxy, check **Use System Network Proxy Preferences** under **Network Proxy** in **Settings → General**. |
| Answers are slow | Pick a smaller or faster model, attach less context, or lower the reasoning effort. Local models slow down with long inputs. |
| One model fails, others work | The problem is with that model or provider. Pick another model in the chat's model picker. |
| Ollama or LM Studio doesn't connect | Make sure the server is running and **BaseUrl** matches its address, then click **Refresh** beside **Model Name**. See [Ollama](/providers/ollama) or [LM Studio](/providers/lm-studio). |

## Shortcuts and windows

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A shortcut does nothing | Another app may use the same keys, such as Spotlight, Raycast, or Alfred. Change one of them in **Settings → Shortcut**. |
| Shortcuts stopped working after an update | Quit and reopen Enconvo. If that doesn't help, turn Accessibility off and on for Enconvo in System Settings. |
| You can't find the app | Enconvo lives in the menu bar. Click its icon, or press **⇧⌘D** for SmartBar. |

## Selected text and the screen

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| PopBar doesn't appear, or Enconvo can't read the selected text | Turn on Accessibility for Enconvo in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. If it's already on, turn it off, wait two seconds, turn it back on, and reopen Enconvo. Secure fields and some custom editors don't share their text. |
| Screenshots, OCR, or screen context fail | Turn on Enconvo in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording**, then reopen Enconvo. |
| A permission is on but has no effect after an update | Remove Enconvo from that list in System Settings with **−**, add it again, and reopen Enconvo. |

## Voice and audio

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Dictation hears nothing | Check Microphone permission, and the microphone selected in **Settings → Dictation**. |
| Dictation is wrong or slow | Try another speech model in **Settings → Dictation Models**. Add names and terms to **Settings → Dictionary**. |
| Read Aloud is silent | Check your Mac's volume and output device. Try **System** in **Settings → Text-to-Speech**; it needs no key and works offline. |

See [Dictation](/features/dictation) and [Speech recognition](/ai/speech-recognition) for more.

## Knowledge Base

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Documents don't finish indexing | Check the embedding provider in **Settings → KnowledgeBase**, and that the file type is supported. |
| Searches find nothing | Make sure the knowledge base is attached to the chat. Lower the score threshold or raise the number of results in the knowledge base's settings. |
| A large knowledge base is slow | Split it into smaller knowledge bases by topic, or add a reranker. |

See [Knowledge Base](/ai/knowledge-base) and [Knowledge Base settings](/advanced/knowledge-base-advanced).

## Browser, apps, and plugins

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Enconvo can't control your browser | Install the Enconvo Companion extension from **Settings → Browser Use**, then restart the browser. See [Browser extension](/features/browser-extension). |
| An Agent can't use your apps | Turn on the permissions **Settings → Computer Use** asks for. See [Computer Use](/features/computer-use). |
| A plugin command fails | Open **Settings → Plugins**, check the plugin's settings and credentials, and update Enconvo. |

## Send diagnostics

When Enconvo freezes, crashes, or keeps misbehaving, send diagnostic logs to the Enconvo team. Choose **Submit Diagnostics…** from the menu bar icon, or open **Settings → Diagnostics**. Enconvo removes personal details, such as file names, email addresses, keys, and the text of your prompts, before anything leaves your Mac, and shows you exactly what was sent. See [Privacy and security](/configuration/privacy-security#analytics-and-diagnostics).

## Get help

- Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/jYsdVRRK2k). Include your Enconvo version (**Settings → About**), your macOS version, what you did, and what happened.
- Report a problem or suggest a feature with **Feedback** in the menu bar menu.
- Check the [FAQ](/faq) for quick answers.
