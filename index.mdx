## Choose your AI

Start with [Enconvo Cloud](/providers/enconvo) for no setup, sign in with a plan you already pay for such as ChatGPT or Claude, add your own API key, or run models on your Mac. [Providers](/providers/overview) compares the options.

## Take it with you

The [iPhone app](/integrations/iphone-app) talks to the Agents on your Mac, and [Phone chats](/integrations/iphone-phone-chats) run on the iPhone itself.
