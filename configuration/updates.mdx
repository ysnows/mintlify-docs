title: "Updates"

---

Enconvo checks for new versions in the background and shows an **Update** button when your update channel has a newer build. Most people stay on Production; switch to Beta to try new features before they're released to everyone.

![Update button in the titlebar](/img/update-button-titlebar.png)

## Update channels

| Channel | Best for | What you get |
|---------|----------|------------------|
| **Production** | Most users | Stable releases after beta testing |
| **Beta** | Early testers | New builds before they're promoted to Production |
| **Dev** | Development testing | More frequent development builds, separate from Beta |

<Tip>
Use **Production** unless you want to test new features early. Use **Beta** if
you're helping test a release or need a fix before the stable release is
published.
</Tip>

## Change your update channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open Enconvo Settings from the app menu, or press **⌘,**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to General">
    Open **General** in the Settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Updates">
    Scroll to the **Updates** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Set **Update Channel** to **Production**, **Beta**, or **Dev**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A fresh Dev installation starts on Dev, and a channel you chose before stays as it is. Switching channels doesn't necessarily downgrade a newer build you already have.

## Install an update

When your channel has a new version, an **Update** button appears in:

- The **Chat Window** titlebar
- The **SmartBar** titlebar
- The **Dynamic Island**

Click **Update**. Enconvo shows the new version and walks you through downloading, installing, and relaunching.

Enconvo checks when you open the Chat Window, SmartBar, or the Dynamic Island. To avoid repeated network requests, it doesn't repeat that check more than once in 10 minutes.

<Note>
Automatic update downloads are off by default. Enconvo can tell you an update
exists and show the button before it downloads anything.
</Note>

## Check now

1. Click the Enconvo icon in the menu bar.
2. Choose **Check for Updates**.
3. If your channel has a newer version, follow the update prompt.

If no update window appears, you may already have the latest version for your channel. To see which version you have, open **Settings → About**: it shows the version, the build, and **Beta** or **Dev** when you're on one of those channels.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You're on Beta but don't see a beta update | Check that **Settings → General → Updates → Update Channel** is **Beta**, then open the Chat Window or SmartBar again. Background checks wait 10 minutes between runs, so give it a few minutes if you just checked. |
| You switched back to Production and the newest build is missing | Production shows only builds published to the stable channel. A build that's still beta-only appears once it's promoted to Production. |
| The **Update** button disappeared | The update may already be installed or dismissed, or may no longer match your channel. Run **Check for Updates** from the menu bar to confirm. |
| The update prompt doesn't open | Quit and reopen Enconvo, then run **Check for Updates** again. Also check that your network can reach Enconvo's release server. |
