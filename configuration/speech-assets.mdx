The Dictionary teaches Enconvo's speech features the product names, technical terms, acronyms, and unusual spellings you use. Speakers keeps the names of the voices you've named in recordings, so new recordings label them for you. Open them from **Settings → Dictionary** and **Settings → Speakers**.

## Add a word

1. Open **Settings → Dictionary** and click **New word**.
2. Enter the preferred spelling, and a short description if it helps.
3. In **Heard as**, optionally enter what Dictation writes instead of this word, separated by commas.
4. Click **Save**, then try a short recording that contains the word.
5. Review the transcription. Provider support and audio quality still affect recognition.

## Add many words at once

In the **Add to dictionary** window, switch to **Batch add** and enter one term per line. Separate a description with `|`:

```text
Enconvo | AI assistant for macOS
Project Atlas | Internal project name
PostgreSQL
```

## Accept suggested words

When you fix a word right after dictating it, the Dictionary lists it under **Suggested from your corrections**. Click **Accept**, and Dictation writes it that way next time, or click **Dismiss**.

## Edit or delete words

Use search and the edit button to keep the list up to date. To delete several words, click **Select**, choose the words, click **Delete**, and confirm.

## Name speakers

1. Open a recording with several speakers.
2. Click a speaker label in the transcript and give that person a name.
3. Enconvo enrolls the voice and recognizes it automatically in new recordings.

Review the speaker assignments in a recording before you rename a speaker. A recognized voice or diarization label can be wrong, especially when people talk over each other.

**Settings → Speakers** lists everyone you've named, with how many recordings they appear in. You can rename or delete a speaker there. Deleting a speaker turns their labels back into speaker numbers; the recordings stay.

## Where they apply

[Dictation](/features/dictation), [Meeting Recording](/features/meeting-recording), [Transcription](/features/transcription), and [Live Captioning](/features/live-captioning) each use their own speech settings. Check each provider's settings for language hints, hot words, diarization, and other options, because providers don't all treat vocabulary the same way.
