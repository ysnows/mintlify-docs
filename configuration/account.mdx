Your Enconvo account holds your plan, your Cloud points, and the Macs that use them. You sign in on **Settings → Account**. The pages under it in the Settings sidebar cover Teams, Sync, Points Usage, Usage Statistics, Affiliate, and Redeem Code. Workspaces are under **Settings → Workspaces**.

This video walks through your account, Teams, Workspaces, and Sync, in that order.

<Note>
An Enconvo account, a provider API key, and a provider subscription sign-in are separate connections. Requests with your own API key use the provider account's billing and limits. Subscription sign-ins show their own usage on the provider's Settings page, separate from Enconvo Cloud points. See [Credentials](/configuration/credentials).
</Note>

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Log In**, or **Sign Up** to create an account.
3. Finish signing in in your browser. If the browser is already signed in, the website asks which account to use. Enconvo brings you back.
4. **Profile** shows the name and email you're signed in with.

While the sign-in finishes, a card in the Chat Window sidebar follows it until your account loads. It says when the network is slow, and offers **Retry** if the account doesn't load. If a sign-in fails or you cancel it, click **Log In** again.

To sign out, click **Log out** at the bottom of the page.

### Change your profile photo

Your profile photo shows to friends, teammates, and people in a conference. If you signed in with Google, your Google photo shows until you pick another.

1. On **Settings → Account**, click **Change photo** next to your photo, or **Add photo** if you have none.
2. Choose **Upload photo…** and pick a picture.
3. Drag the photo to place it in the circle, zoom with the slider, and click **Save**.

The same menu has **Use Google photo** to go back to your Google photo, and **Remove photo** to show your initial instead.

### Add an email or a password

The **Sign-in** section on **Settings → Account** shows how your account signs in. If you signed up with Apple and Hide My Email, your email shows as hidden by Apple.

1. Click **Add email**, enter your own email, and enter the code sent to it. You can then sign in with that email.
2. Click **Set password** to sign in with your email and password on enconvo.com and Enconvo Feedback. If you already have one, the button is **Reset password**, and it doesn't ask for the old password.

The same section is in the iPhone and Android apps.

## Check your plan and points

**Settings → Account** shows what your account has:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Points** | Your **Total balance**. **View usage** opens Points Usage, and **Top Up** buys more points. |
| **Lifetime Licence** | Your license tier, **Standard** or **Premium**, when you bought it, how long updates and support last, and your devices. Without a license, **Get Lifetime** shows the options, and **Start trial** appears when a free Standard trial is offered. |
| **Teams** | Your Seats, when you're a Team admin, with **Manage team**. |
| **Enconvo Cloud** | Your Cloud tier, billing period, next billing date, when points reset, and your devices. **Get Cloud plan** or **Upgrade** changes it, and **View pricing** opens the model and service rates. |

At the bottom, **Manage billing** opens your billing details in the browser. While a lifetime purchase can still be refunded, **Request refund** appears there too. A refund returns the account to the free plan and reverses its bonus points.

### Devices

Each plan covers a set number of Macs, and **Devices** shows how many you use. When you reach the limit, the page says **Device limit reached** and this Mac can't run commands. Click **Manage devices**, remove a Mac you no longer use, then activate this one, or choose a plan with more devices.

## Teams

Teams lets you buy Seats and give each one to a person by email. Everyone signs in with their own account, and you can share Cloud points with them. Only a Team admin sees **Teams** in the Settings sidebar.

1. Open **Settings → Teams**. The top shows **Seats assigned**, **Free seats**, and **Top-up points**.
2. Under **Assign a Seat**, enter the email the person signs in to Enconvo with, and click **Assign**.
3. The Seat shows **Pending** until they first sign in to Enconvo, then **Active**.

Their account gets Premium features and lifetime updates on one Mac, and keeps its chats, memory, and knowledge bases private. You see Seats and point totals only.

Each member's **Seat actions** menu has:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Transfer points** | Moves points from your top-up balance to theirs. They never expire. |
| **Take back points** | Moves points they haven't spent back to your top-up balance. |
| **Unassign Seat** | Returns their account to Free right away. It keeps its data, and transferred points come back to you. For your own Seat, this is **Give up your Seat**. |

Each Seat is one Mac. Click **+** on a row to let that account, yours included, sign in on more Macs. **Add seats** buys more.

**Team knowledge bases** lists the knowledge bases shared with everyone on your Team. To share one, open its **⋯** menu and turn on Team access. A member's knowledge bases stay private until they share one with the Team.

## Workspaces

A Workspace keeps work for a client or a job apart from your own. Each Workspace has its own chats, Memory, knowledge, sign-ins, and settings, and nothing in one shows up in another. A setting a Workspace never changes follows **Personal**, your own work.

The video below makes a Workspace for a client, switches back to Personal, and manages Workspaces in Settings.

<YouTubePlayer videoId="fMHIhDRufXA" title="Workspaces in Enconvo" />

### Create a Workspace

1. At the top of the Chat Window sidebar, click the Workspace menu, which shows **Personal**, and choose **New Workspace…**. You can also click **New Workspace…** in **Settings → Workspaces**.
2. The first time, a short guide, **What is a Workspace?**, explains three things: **Keep work apart**, **Switch at the top of the sidebar**, and **Starts from Personal**. Click **Continue**.
3. Enter a **Workspace name**, such as the client you work for, and click **Create**.

The Chat Window opens in the new Workspace. Its chats, Memory, and knowledge start empty, and nothing from Personal shows up there.

### Switch Workspaces

Click the Workspace menu at the top of the Chat Window sidebar and pick a Workspace. Or press **⌃⌘1** to **⌃⌘9**: the number is the Workspace's place in the list, so **⌃⌘1** is Personal.

Each window works in one Workspace, and work in several Workspaces can run at once.

### What stays apart

Each Workspace has its own chats, Memory, knowledge, sign-ins, and settings. Coding agent chats stay inside the Workspace they started in, and a new [Space](/features/spaces) keeps separate data in each Workspace. A new Workspace uses your Personal settings until you change one in it.

To give a Connector its own account in a Workspace, switch to that Workspace, open the Connector on the Plugins page, and choose **Make default** in the account's **⋯** menu. The account shows **Default in** and the Workspace's name, and the agent uses it there unless you name another account in your request.

If you're a Team admin, the **Teams** page lets you put each member in one or more of your Workspaces. Members then see, chat with, and call only the teammates who share a Workspace with them.

### Rename or delete a Workspace

Open **Settings → Workspaces**. **Personal** is always first: its settings are the ones every Workspace starts from, and it can't be renamed or deleted.

Each other Workspace's row has **Rename** and **Delete**. Deleting a Workspace takes its chats, Memory, knowledge, and settings with it. It stays under **Recently deleted** for 7 days, where **Restore** brings it back.

You can also ask an agent to list, create, rename, recolor, reorder, delete, or restore your Workspaces. It confirms before it deletes one.

## Sync your Macs

Sync keeps your chats, settings, skills, plugins, knowledge bases, and artifacts the same on every computer signed in to your account. It's part of Enconvo Cloud plans and lifetime licenses.

1. Sign in with the same Enconvo account on each computer.
2. Open **Settings → Sync** and turn on **Sync across computers**.
3. If Enconvo asks where to keep what it syncs, choose your **Enconvo account** or **iCloud**. You can change this later under **Storage**.
4. Under **What syncs**, choose what to include.

| Item | What syncs | What stays on this computer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chats** | Chat history with its pictures and files, titles, projects, and archived chats | |
| **Settings** | Models, prompts, plugin preferences, your snippets, and the AI commands, agents, and workflows you create | Sign-ins, hotkeys, and folder paths |
| **Skills** | Skills you installed or made, and whether each is turned on. A skill installed from GitHub keeps its source, so your other computers check it for updates too | Skills that come with plugins stay with them |
| **Plugins** | Plugins you installed from the Enconvo store, GitHub, npm, or a link: your other computers install them too, and uninstall what you uninstall | Built-in plugins, plugins you're developing, Connectors, and sign-ins |
| **Knowledge Bases** | Your knowledge bases and Memory, with their notes, text, and web pages | Imported files and recordings |
| **Artifacts** | Your [artifacts](/features/artifacts) with every version: pages, documents, designs, slides, dashboards, pictures, and videos. When two Macs change one at the same time, both versions are kept | Files over 32 MB, and shared docs and designs |
| **API Keys** | API keys for built-in providers, encrypted before they leave this computer | Sign-ins and custom providers |

Passwords and sign-ins never leave the computer. With your Enconvo account as storage, a new computer gets your API keys only after one of your other computers allows it: check that both show the same code, then click **Allow**. A **Backup passphrase** lets a computer get your API keys when none of your other computers is at hand. With iCloud, your Macs signed in to the same iCloud get the keys on their own.

**Sync Now** syncs right away, and **Your computers** lists every computer that syncs. **Clear synced data** deletes everything synced to your account or iCloud, from every computer. What's on your computers stays, and Sync turns off on this computer and pauses on the others until you turn it on again.

## Points Usage and Usage Statistics

**Settings → Points Usage** shows your Enconvo Cloud points:

- **Total balance**: everything you can spend now. Subscription points are used first.
- **Subscription balance**: your monthly or yearly allowance, reset to the plan amount on each renewal.
- **Top-up balance**: points from top-up packs and lifetime licenses. They never expire.
- A daily usage chart by model, and the **History** of each charge.

In **History**, filter by **Record type** and **Model**, pick a day with **Jump to date**, and see the points used each day.

**Settings → Usage Statistics** shows cost, tokens, and requests across every AI provider Enconvo calls, your own keys included. Switch between **Cost**, **Tokens**, and **Requests**, pick **24h**, **7d**, **30d**, or **90d**, and break the numbers down by model, provider, or feature. Costs are estimates at the providers' API list prices.

## Affiliate

**Settings → Affiliate** gives you your link and code; **Affiliate…** in the menu bar menu opens it too. Earlier versions call this page **Invite Friends**. Share them with **Copy link** or **Copy code**. Each person has one link, `enconvo.com/i/<your code>`.

- **Your friend** gets 250,000 Cloud points ($5) when they sign up with your link or code and then sign in to the Enconvo app on a device that's new to Enconvo. Signing in on the web doesn't count.
- **You** get 20% of your friend's first payment, in Cloud points (50,000 points = $1), when they pay within 30 days of signing up. A yearly plan counts as its yearly price divided by 12.
- A plan's share is credited right away. A lifetime license's share waits 30 days, its refund window; a refund in that time cancels it.
- Only the payment counts, not the device or app your friend pays from.
- If your friend opens someone else's link before paying, the last link they opened gets the reward. A promotion code entered at checkout wins over any link, and one payment never pays both points and an Affiliate commission.

Invite codes can't be used from temporary or agent email addresses, and there's no limit on how many friends you can invite. **Your invites** shows each friend and where your share stands: waiting for their first payment, in the refund window, or earned.

If a friend invited you, enter their code under **Got an invite code?** and click **Redeem**.

### Earn cash instead

Bloggers, creators, and anyone with an audience can apply to the [Affiliate program](https://www.enconvo.com/affiliate), with or without the app. Once approved, the same link earns a 20% cash commission on every payment from the people you refer, renewals included. Subscription commissions are payable right away, and lifetime license commissions after their 30-day refund window. Payouts go out on the 1st of each month by PayPal or Wise, once your balance reaches $50; smaller balances roll over. Points you earned before approval stay yours. If your application isn't approved, you can apply again after 60 days. The full rules are in the [Affiliate program terms](https://www.enconvo.com/affiliate/terms).

## Redeem a code

1. Open **Settings → Redeem Code**.
2. Paste the code, or the link it came in, and click **Continue**.

License codes and no-card trials activate on your account right away. A Teams License code makes your account the Team admin of the code's Seats, with 50,000 Cloud points per Seat; see [Teams](#teams). A trial that needs a card opens checkout in your browser and shows its renewal terms before you confirm.

## About You

To tell your agents about yourself, open **Settings → About You**. All your agents, voice calls, and the Avatar use what you write there, so only add what you want used to personalize their replies.
