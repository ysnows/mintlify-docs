Your Enconvo account holds your plan, your Cloud points, and the Macs that use them. You sign in on **Settings → Account**. The pages under it in the Settings sidebar cover Teams, Sync, Points Usage, Usage Statistics, Invite Friends, and Redeem Code.

<Note>
An Enconvo account, a provider API key, and a provider subscription sign-in are separate connections. Requests with your own API key use the provider account's billing and limits. Subscription sign-ins show their own usage on the provider's Settings page, separate from Enconvo Cloud points. See [Credentials](/configuration/credentials).
</Note>

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Log In**, or **Sign Up** to create an account.
3. Finish signing in in your browser. Enconvo brings you back.
4. **Profile** shows the name and email you're signed in with.

To sign out, click **Log out** at the bottom of the page.

## Check your plan and points

**Settings → Account** shows what your account has:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Points** | Your **Total balance**. **View usage** opens Points Usage, and **Top Up** buys more points. |
| **Lifetime Licence** | Your license tier, when you bought it, how long updates and support last, and your devices. Without a license, **Get Lifetime** shows the options, and **Start trial** appears when a free Plus trial is offered. |
| **Teams** | Your Seats, when you're a Team admin, with **Manage team**. |
| **Enconvo Cloud** | Your Cloud tier, billing period, next billing date, when points reset, and your devices. **Get Cloud plan** or **Upgrade** changes it, and **View pricing** opens the model and service rates. |

At the bottom, **Manage billing** opens your billing details in the browser. While a lifetime purchase can still be refunded, **Request refund** appears there too. A refund returns the account to the free plan and reverses its bonus points.

### Devices

Each plan covers a set number of Macs, and **Devices** shows how many you use. When you reach the limit, the page says **Device limit reached** and this Mac can't run commands. Click **Manage devices**, remove a Mac you no longer use, then activate this one, or choose a plan with more devices.

## Teams

Teams lets you buy Seats and give each one to a person by email. Everyone signs in with their own account, and you can share Cloud points with them. Only a Team admin sees **Teams** in the Settings sidebar.

1. Open **Settings → Teams**. The top shows **Seats assigned**, **Free seats**, and **Top-up points**.
2. Under **Assign a Seat**, enter the email the person signs in to Enconvo with, and click **Assign**.
3. The Seat shows **Pending** until they first sign in to Enconvo, then **Active**.

Their account gets Premium features and lifetime updates on one Mac, and keeps its chats, memory, and knowledge bases private. You see Seats and point totals only.

Each member's **Seat actions** menu has:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Transfer points** | Moves points from your top-up balance to theirs. They never expire. |
| **Take back points** | Moves points they haven't spent back to your top-up balance. |
| **Unassign Seat** | Returns their account to Free right away. It keeps its data, and transferred points come back to you. For your own Seat, this is **Give up your Seat**. |

Each Seat is one Mac. Click **+** on a row to let that account, yours included, sign in on more Macs. **Add seats** buys more.

## Sync your Macs

Sync keeps your chats, settings, and knowledge bases the same on every computer signed in to your account. It's part of Enconvo Cloud plans and lifetime licenses.

1. Sign in with the same Enconvo account on each computer.
2. Open **Settings → Sync** and turn on **Sync across computers**.
3. If Enconvo asks where to keep what it syncs, choose your **Enconvo account** or **iCloud**. You can change this later under **Storage**.
4. Under **What syncs**, choose what to include.

| Item | What syncs | What stays on this computer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chats** | Chat history with its pictures and files, titles, projects, and archived chats | |
| **Settings** | Models, prompts, plugin preferences, your skills and snippets, and the AI commands, agents, and workflows you create | Sign-ins, hotkeys, and folder paths |
| **Knowledge Bases** | Your knowledge bases and Memory, with their notes, text, and web pages | Imported files and recordings |
| **API Keys** | API keys for built-in providers, encrypted before they leave this computer | Sign-ins and custom providers |

Passwords and sign-ins never leave the computer. With your Enconvo account as storage, a new computer gets your API keys only after one of your other computers allows it: check that both show the same code, then click **Allow**. A **Backup passphrase** lets a computer get your API keys when none of your other computers is at hand. With iCloud, your Macs signed in to the same iCloud get the keys on their own.

**Sync Now** syncs right away, and **Your computers** lists every computer that syncs. **Clear synced data** deletes everything synced to your account or iCloud, from every computer. What's on your computers stays, and Sync turns off on this computer and pauses on the others until you turn it on again.

## Points Usage and Usage Statistics

**Settings → Points Usage** shows your Enconvo Cloud points:

- **Total balance**: everything you can spend now. Subscription points are used first.
- **Subscription balance**: your monthly or yearly allowance, reset to the plan amount on each renewal.
- **Top-up balance**: points from top-up packs and lifetime licenses. They never expire.
- A daily usage chart by model, and the **History** of each charge.

**Settings → Usage Statistics** shows cost, tokens, and requests across every AI provider Enconvo calls, your own keys included. Switch between **Cost**, **Tokens**, and **Requests**, pick **24h**, **7d**, **30d**, or **90d**, and break the numbers down by model, provider, or feature. Costs are estimates at the providers' API list prices.

## Invite friends

**Settings → Invite Friends** gives you an invite link and code. Share them with **Copy link** or **Copy code**.

When a friend signs up with your invite and then signs in to the Enconvo app on a device that's new to Enconvo, you each get points. There's no limit on how many friends you can invite, and signing in on the web doesn't count. **Your invites** shows who joined and what you've earned.

If a friend invited you, enter their code under **Got an invite code?** and click **Redeem**.

## Redeem a code

1. Open **Settings → Redeem Code**.
2. Paste the code, or the link it came in, and click **Continue**.

License codes and no-card trials activate on your account right away. A trial that needs a card opens checkout in your browser and shows its renewal terms before you confirm.

## About You

To tell your agents about yourself, open **Settings → About You**. All your agents, voice calls, and the Avatar use what you write there, so only add what you want used to personalize their replies.
